## Quick answer

The first broken engine near the Foreman/Snowman, toward the 10G antenna.

## Location

The first broken engine near the Foreman/Snowman, toward the 10G antenna.

## Materials needed

Per engine: Electrical Tape x2, Aluminum x2, Rubber x2.

## Steps

1. Repair the small engines with the materials (three total)
2. Police appear when you fix the last one — run away

## Reward

Unlocks the Boosters equipment recipe (flight speed).
