## Quick answer

The strange toilet near the 10G antenna/memorial and Chuck's Taxi station.

## Location

The strange toilet near the 10G antenna/memorial and Chuck's Taxi station.

## Materials needed

Battery; Panel: Rubber x2, Techbits x2, Aluminum x4; Wiring: Plastic x2, Refined Metal x2, Electronics x2.

## Steps

1. Break the door beams with the Scrap tool
2. Place the battery in the slot
3. Repair both sides: Panel and Wiring
4. Open the door for the seat collectible, press the red button

## Reward

Weird toilet achievement + Cold Protection +10.
