## Quick answer

**The demo was scrapped and rebuilt.** The 2025 Next Fest demo's content (train door code, taxi quest, etc.) no longer applies in Early Access, and demo saves don't carry over.

## Demo vs Early Access

The developers stated multiple times that the 2025 demo was **thrown out and rebuilt from scratch**. The Early Access build (2026-08-31) is almost entirely new. Therefore:

- Demo-era guides (door codes, old recipes) are **mostly invalid**
- Demo saves **do not carry over** to EA
- Use Early Access (2026-08-31) guides as the source of truth

## Why some guides don't match

If an online guide doesn't match your game, it was likely written for the demo. Only trust guides marked "Early Access / 2026-08-31".
