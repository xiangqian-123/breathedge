Breathedge 2 is an ironic space survival comedy — the developers call it "Subnautica in space, with a chicken and a bunch of stupid jokes." You play as the Man, waking up near a crashed space station, surviving while pushing the main story forward. This guide covers the points where new players most often get stuck.

## The four survival stats

The game tracks four stats you must manage at all times:

- **Oxygen**: refills automatically inside the ship; outside, use oxygen stations, Oxyworms, or oxygen tanks. Ice is the raw material for the oxygen machine.
- **Hunger**: raw food like apples only helps a little; the real fix is the Nutri-Pack-O-Mat.
- **Thirst**: the Kettle is your only stable water source; you can also chew ice, but ice is also your oxygen source — don't waste it.
- **Rest**: sleep in the bed on the ship to recover, at no cost.

## Starting tool chain (in order)

Tools unlock in a gather → mine → cut sequence; skip a step and you're stuck:

1. **Crowbar**: crafted from starter Metal, used to break locks and dismantle wreckage.
2. **Cutter**: Plastic + Aluminum + Metal, unlocks more materials.
3. **Thumper**: Metal + Plastic, used to mine aluminum ore from asteroids.

> Tip: blueprints unlock in two ways — the first time you pick up a resource, or through story/quest progression. Whenever a new material appears (Fabric, Oxyworm, Refined Metal, Light Bulb), grab at least one to trigger new recipes.

## Fix these two machines first

Food and water come first. Prioritize these two machines:

- **Kettle**: your only stable water source; feed it ice to produce water.
- **Nutri-Pack-O-Mat**: each Nutri-Pack restores about 25 hunger, far more than raw food's 6.

Both need Electrical Tape to repair — it spawns on the red pipes at the train platform edge, and needs the Cutter to harvest.

## Common beginner mistakes

- **Don't linger outside**: oxygen runs out; loop back to the ship or an oxygen station.
- **Stockpile plastic**: lots of repairs need plastic — salvage it whenever you see it.
- **Don't rush ahead**: some areas are blocked by missing gear, enemies, or obstacles; make your tools first.

> ⚠️ The game is in Early Access (launched Aug 31, 2026); mechanics and values may change with updates — check in-game.
