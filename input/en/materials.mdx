Every confirmed resource in Chapter 1, grouped into base materials, advanced materials, biological materials, and fuel, with where to find them.

## Base materials (10)

| Material | Where to find it |
|------|------|
| **Metal** | Floating metal orbs in space, old washing machines and other wreckage |
| **Plastic** | Salvaged from wreckage; takes several hits to drop |
| **Aluminum** | Asteroid ore nodes, mined with the Thumper |
| **Glass** | Shards / wreckage |
| **Wires** | Electronic devices, TVs |
| **Electronics** | Old TVs and devices |
| **Duct Tape** | Wreckage / exploration pickups |
| **Electrical Tape** | Red pipes at the train platform edge (~4 pipes, 2 units each, needs Cutter) |
| **Fabric** | First pickup unlocks the Medkit recipe |
| **Light Bulb** | Unscrew from light fixtures |

## Advanced materials

| Material | Where to find it |
|------|------|
| **Refined Metal** | Frozen areas |
| **Titanium** | Further regions (late game; for Backpack / Spraytron) |
| **Rubber** | TBC (needed to repair engines, ~4 units) |

## Biological materials

| Material | Notes |
|------|------|
| **Oxyworm** | Floating worm; makes Purified Oxygen / Oxygen Tank; can be sucked for oxygen |
| **Nutriworm** | Used to craft Alcohol |

## Fuel & other

| Material | Notes |
|------|------|
| **Fuel** | Vehicle / engine related |
| **Ice** | Raw material for the oxygen machine and Kettle; also chewable for water |

## Gathering tips

- Grab at least one of any new material to unlock recipes.
- **Plastic is essential**: many repairs need it — salvage it whenever you see it.
- **Electrical Tape is easy to miss**: it's on the half-damaged red pipes at the train platform edge, blending into the industrial background — search the edges, not just the top.

> ⚠️ Early Access resource locations may change — check in-game.
