## Quick answer

**Knobs: first down, second up, third right.** The hint is the word Racket with arrows on the back of the Tickets building. A Token drops out when correct.

## Steps

1. Check the "Racket" + arrows on the back of the Tickets building
2. At the front, turn the first knob down
3. Second up, third right
4. A Token drops out when correct
