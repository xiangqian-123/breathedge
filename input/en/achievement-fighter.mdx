## Quick answer

Head to the bus depot and interact with the window beside Granny. Attempt to force the window open three times to trigger the achievement.

## Official description

> Only a real man would dare oppose a granny on a bus.

## Reward

Unlocking it permanently grants **Cold Protection +5**. Achievement bonuses stack, and their effects can be checked on the in-game Rewards screen.
