## Quick answer

The playground near the Hubble screens.

## Location

The playground near the Hubble screens.

## Materials needed

None (move the yellow mannequins).

## Steps

1. Approach the playground to trigger the hide-the-mannequins task
2. Find the 4 yellow kid mannequins and put them into lockers (left of lockers, near the slide, right edge, and above the lockers)

## Reward

Safe content achievement + Radiation Protection +10.
