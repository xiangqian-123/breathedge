Breathedge 2 launched to good reception — **"Very Positive"** on Steam with 431 reviews at the time of writing.

## What players and outlets praise

- **Absurd humor**: the first game's signature deadpan comedy continues — "Subnautica in space + chicken + stupid jokes."
- **DIY crafting**: the seven-category module system (Sharp, Impact, Protective, etc.) adds real depth over the original.
- **Clear crafting chain**: Crowbar → Cutter → Thumper gives a satisfying sense of progression.
- **Visuals & atmosphere**: retro-futuristic style with colorful planets stands out.

## Caveats

- **Incomplete content**: Early Access has only Chapter 1; the full game is 16-20 months away.
- **Oxygen limits**: early exploration is tethered to a tight radius, forcing frequent returns to refill.
- **Demo was rebuilt**: the developers admitted the original demo "masked the absence of mechanics with dialogue," and rebuilt it toward survival and exploration.

## Verdict

If you like survival + crafting + absurd comedy, Chapter 1 is worth trying. If you want the full story and stable balance, wait for 1.0 — but expect a higher price.

> Reception changes with updates; the above reflects Early Access launch.
