## Quick answer

Behind the train, near the mole-burrow asteroids.

## Location

Behind the train, near the mole-burrow asteroids.

## Materials needed

Electrical Tape x3, Metal x2.

## Steps

1. Fly over the train to find the disconnected loudspeaker
2. Carry it back and attach it to the train
3. Repair with the materials
4. Click once more to turn it on

## Reward

Increases the Oxyworms you'll find in the area.
