## Quick answer

**Alkali** is found in **Alkaline Mole burrows** inside hollow asteroids. Get it with the **Spraytron** (yield 3-5 per deposit), a **mole milker** (continuous supply), or pick it up by hand (rare).

## Where to find Alkali

Alkaline Mole burrows sit inside **hollow asteroids** — look for the mole burrows and extract from them.

## How to get Alkali

| Method | Yield |
| --- | --- |
| **Spraytron** extraction | 3-5 per deposit |
| **Mole milker** | continuous supply |
| **Hand pick-up** | rare |

## What Alkali is used for

Alkali is the raw material for **Alkaline Solution** — the liquid that fills the shuttle in the Signal Jammer quest. It's also reported in a few other recipes (Battery Cell, Leather Bag, Pounding Rig) — confirm exact recipes in-game.
