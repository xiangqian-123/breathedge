## Quick answer

Find the clay sculpture on the **three-toilet platform** (fly up left of Hubble), collect 3 clay pieces and reattach them to earn the **Go down in history** achievement + Maximum Satiety +10.

## Location

The platform with three toilets (fly up to the left of Hubble).

## Materials needed

[Scrap](/en/guide/recipe-scrap) tool (break yellow ice for clay).

## Steps

1. Find the clay sculpture
2. Collect 3 clay pieces (beside the sculpture, inside the first port-a-potty, under the platform by breaking yellow ice)
3. Bring them back, attach, and sculpt

## Patch 0.8.10 changes

- The quest now shows **3 markers**, one per missing clay piece — they **disappear one by one** as you collect each piece (helps if you were stuck finding the last one).
- The **mud ball trajectory now points at the sculpture center**, so you can see exactly where to throw and attach.

## Reward

Go down in history achievement + Maximum Satiety +10.
