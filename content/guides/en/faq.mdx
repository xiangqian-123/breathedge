The most common questions, grouped into purchase & platforms, game content, and gameplay.

## Purchase & platforms

### 1. When does Breathedge 2 release?

It entered Early Access on **Aug 31, 2026** on Steam and Epic Games Store.

→ See [Release Date](/en/guide/release-date)

### 2. How much does it cost?

US Steam price: $19.99 base, **$15.99** with the -20% launch discount (until Sep 14). The price will rise with updates and at full release.

→ See [Price](/en/guide/price)

### 3. What platforms is it on?

Currently **Windows PC** only (Steam + Epic). PS5 and Xbox are TBA.

→ See [Platforms](/en/guide/platforms)

### 4. Is there multiplayer or co-op?

**No.** It's strictly single-player — no co-op or multiplayer.

### 5. Does it support Chinese?

Yes — interface text includes **Simplified Chinese** (13 languages total); audio is English and Russian.

### 6. Are the system requirements high?

No. Minimum GTX 1660 / RX 5500 XT, 12GB RAM, 15GB storage.

→ See [System Requirements](/en/guide/system-requirements)

## Game content

### 7. What does Early Access include?

Chapter 1 of the main story plus one large explorable area, with survival, crafting, upgrades, combat, and quests. Later chapters arrive with updates.

### 8. When is the full release?

Unannounced. Early Access is expected to last **16-20 months**, but that window may move.

### 9. Is the old demo still available?

The demo was **rebuilt from scratch**, so old demo guides (train door code, taxi quest) may no longer apply — follow the EA version.

## Gameplay

### 10. How do I get stable drinking water?

Fix the **Kettle** (feed it ice). Without it you can only loot water packs or chew ice — but ice is also your oxygen source, so don't waste it.

→ See [Beginner Guide](/en/guide/beginner)

### 11. How do I refill oxygen?

It refills **inside the ship automatically**; outside, use **oxygen stations**, **Oxyworms**, or an **oxygen tank**.

→ See [Survival Systems](/en/guide/survival)

### 12. How do I unlock new recipes?

Two ways: **picking up a resource for the first time**, or **story/quest progression**. Grab at least one of any new material.

→ See [Crafting & Upgrades](/en/guide/crafting)

### 13. Is the starter tool "Crowbar" or "Scrap"?

The official announcement and most guides call it the **Crowbar** (for breaking locks and salvaging); a few guides write "Scrap." Go by the in-game name.
