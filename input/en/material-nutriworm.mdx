## Quick answer

**Nutriworm** spawns near the **frozen green nutrient tank above Chuck's Taxi**, roughly 8-12 at a time.

## Where to find it

Head to the Chuck's Taxi area and locate the **frozen green nutrient tank above it**. A batch of Nutriworms (about 8-12) spawns nearby.

> ⚠️ Location is a single-source lead, not yet multi-source verified — check in-game. If you can't find them, search around the nutrient tank and related equipment above the taxi first.

## What it's used for

Nutriworm is the key ingredient for crafting **Alcohol**:

| Recipe | Materials |
| --- | --- |
| Alcohol | Ice x2 + Nutriworm x1 |

Alcohol is then used to craft the **Medkit**.
