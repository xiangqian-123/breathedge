Breathedge 2 achievements are unusual: **completing one grants a stat bonus** rather than just a trophy. They come from main quests, side quests, and random encounters. Here are the confirmed Chapter 1 achievements by category.

## Exploration achievements

| Achievement | How to unlock | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **Ladies' man** | Return the missing glove across the railroad to the suited corpse at the end of the rails | Max Thirst +10 |
| **Prankster** | Get the phone, call the anti-terrorism hotline, pick option 3, stay on for ~5 minutes | Max Oxygen +10 |
| **Safe content** | Move the 4 yellow mannequins around the playground into lockers and close them | Radiation Protection +10 |
| **Visitor** | Physically enter all 7 toilets in the Outskirts | Fatigue Drain -5% |
| **Hunting Instinct** | Pull 10 moles out with Sui's mechanical arm | Carry Weight +10 |
| **Jacked** | Interact with the urological exercise machine 10 times | Flight Speed +5% |
| **Traditional Values** | Destroy the love locks near the train debris with the Crowbar | Max Health +10 |
| **Consistency** | Place the floating number 4 onto the incomplete sign outside the station | Oxygen Consumption -5% |

## Side quest & NPC achievements

| Achievement | How to unlock | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **Attendant** | Repair the damaged Attendant near the train station with her missing body parts | Physical Protection +2 |
| **Gladiator** | Find the Bum, start the Battle for the Mattress, and beat him across the rounds | Max Health +10 |

## Consumable achievements

| Achievement | How to unlock | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **Happy whatever!** | Drink 50 bottles of Alcohol in total | Radiation Protection +10 |
| **Hehehehehe** | Drink 10 bottles of Alcohol back-to-back | Thirst Drain -5% |
| **Slave to the Stomach** | Recycle 100 nutrients through the Nutri-Pack-O-Mat | Hunger Drain -5% |

## Gear achievements

| Achievement | How to unlock | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **Curious** | Remove your helmet in open space (put it back immediately) | Flight Speed +5% |
| **Conehead** | Find the traffic cone helmet and wear it | Fatigue Drain -5% |
| **Honorary vandal** | Destroy 200 valid objects with the Crowbar (cumulative) | Damage +5 |

## Other Chapter 1 achievements

| Achievement | How to unlock | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **Shipbuilder** | Repair the rocket toilet off the rails and launch the Thronehauler | Oxygen Consumption -5% |
| **System Administrator** | Reach the PornHubble, destroy the 4 small caches by the main panel, then the large one opposite | Cold Protection +5 |
| **Weird toilet** | Find the unusual toilet installation and repair its parts | Cold Protection +10 |
| **Leader** | Rebuild the Leader statue with its missing colored pieces | Gas Reserve +20 |
| **Oxyworm Collector** | Collect 300 Oxyworms total (cumulative) | Max Oxygen +30 |
| **Now That's a Snowman!** | Raise the temperature, follow the yellow pipes, fix the gas lamp, clear ice, rebuild the snowman | Cold Protection +10 |

> ⚠️ Achievements and rewards may change with updates — check in-game. Clear multiple exploration achievements in the same area (police station, bus depot, railroad, platforms) to avoid backtracking.
