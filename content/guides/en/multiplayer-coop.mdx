## Quick answer

**No.** Breathedge 2 is a single-player only game — there is no online multiplayer and no co-op mode.

## Steam store status

The Steam store page lists Breathedge 2 as **Single-player** only, with no Multi-player, Online Co-op, or online play tags.

## What the developers said

In the Early Access description, the developers state that Breathedge 2 is a single-player, story-driven survival game with **no current multiplayer or co-op plans**. The developer reply to the Steam Community "online coop?" thread confirms the same: no co-op is planned.

## Will multiplayer be added later?

No multiplayer or co-op roadmap has been announced. Early Access is expected to last roughly 16-20 months; if that changes, it will come through official announcements. Don't expect co-op right now.

## Is there cross-play?

No. With no multiplayer mode at all, cross-platform play doesn't apply.

## What about the first Breathedge?

The original Breathedge was also single-player only and never added multiplayer. The sequel continues that design.
