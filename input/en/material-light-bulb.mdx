## Quick answer

Light Bulbs come from **working lamps** — only the **blinking red ones** have bulbs. Dive **straight down below the station** to the circular pipe structure with the red border, then use the **Twister**: unscrew the clamp, pull it, and pry open the round cover.

## Where to find light bulbs

### 1. The circular pipe structure below the station

From the station/module, **dive straight down** (don't fly out along the platform). You'll find a large circular pipe structure with a red border — the **blinking red lamps** inside it hold bulbs.

### 2. Red signal lights in the yellow train tunnel

The **yellow train tunnel** below the platform has red signal lights that work the same way.

## Which lamps have bulbs?

Only **blinking** red lights contain bulbs. Constantly-lit lamps are empty — opening them wastes oxygen for nothing.

## Required tool: Twister

The **Twister** is mandatory — lamps are scenery without it.

- **Unlock gate**: the Twister blueprint is locked behind the **PornHubble antenna** quest (part of the three-antenna chain). Until then, lamps show no interaction prompt at all.
- Two-step action: **Unscrew the clamp → pull and pry open the round cover**. Stopping after the first step gives you nothing.

## What light bulbs are used for

| Use | Light Bulbs needed |
| --- | --- |
| **Flashlight** (Plastic x2 + Wires x2 + Glass x2 + Light Bulb x1) | x1 |
| **Service Warmer repair** (Metal x4 + Wires x4 + Electronics x2 + Light Bulb x1) | x1 |

The Flashlight recipe unlocks when you obtain your **first bulb**. You'll need more than one bulb during Chapter 1.

## Achievement bonus

The **Hoodlum** achievement: unscrew **10 bulbs** in total → **Heat Protection +5**.

## Respawn

No timer or mechanic confirmed. The pipe structure is a repeatable supply point only in the sense that uncollected lamps stay there — don't expect emptied lamps to refill.

## FAQ

### 1. Where is the Light Bulb in Breathedge 2?
Blinking red lamps in the circular pipe structure directly below the station, plus red signal lights in the yellow train tunnel.

### 2. Why can't I interact with any lamp?
You need the Twister, which unlocks after the PornHubble antenna quest. Before that, lamps give no prompt.

### 3. The lamp opened but nothing came out.
Only blinking red lights have bulbs — constantly-lit lamps are empty.

### 4. What tool do I use for Light Bulbs?
The Twister: unscrew the clamp, pull, and pry open the cover — both steps, not just the first.

### 5. Do Light Bulbs respawn?
Not confirmed. Treat each lamp as one-time.
