## Quick answer

Orange metal wreckage floating in space; also drops from breaking barrels and metal containers.

## How to get it

Break down wreckage and containers with the [Crowbar (Scrap)](/en/guide/recipe-scrap).

![Breathedge 2 gameplay — breaking a Metal ball with the Break prompt](/images/guides/metal-ball.jpg)

## What it's used for

| Use | Metal needed |
| --- | --- |
| [Scrap (Crowbar)](/en/guide/recipe-scrap) | x2 |
| [Cutter](/en/guide/recipe-cutter) | x4 |
| [Thumper](/en/guide/recipe-thumper) | x4 |
| [Purified Oxygen](/en/guide/recipe-purified-oxygen) | x1 |
| [Oxygen Station repair](/en/guide/quest-public-oxygen-station) | x3 |

> Metal and [Refined Metal](/en/guide/material-refined-metal) are **separate resources** — Refined Metal is not smelted from Metal.
