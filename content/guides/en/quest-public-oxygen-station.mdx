## Quick answer

The Public Oxygen Station is **left of the train platform, opposite the Attendant platform**. Repair it with **Metal x3 + Electronics x2 + Wires x1 + Electrical Tape x3**, then use it up close — it refills your oxygen, and you keep it supplied **by filling it with ice, same as the train**.

## Location

Station Outskirts — **left of the train platform, directly across from the Attendant platform**. The Attendant's head piece is also in this corner, so the area is easy to recognize.

![Breathedge 2 gameplay — the "Repair the Oxygen Station" task prompt](/images/guides/oxygen-station-repair.jpg)

## How to get there

The station only becomes interactive after you **restore the train's power and raise the pantograph** — that step thaws the surrounding ice and activates dead equipment. If you have all the materials but nothing happens when you approach, advance the main story or repair nearby equipment and come back.

## Materials needed

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Metal | x3 |
| Electronics | x2 |
| Wires | x1 |
| Electrical Tape | x3 |

> Electronics and Electrical Tape are also eaten by nearby machine repairs — bring the full list with you.

## How to use it after repairing

- Walk up to the station to refill oxygen — use it up close, like the train's oxygen terminal
- The supply is **finite**: refill it with **Ice**, same as the train
- Treat it as a forward base: fill up → complete one nearby objective → return to refill → take the next branch
- Don't process all your Ice into water — keep a few pieces for oxygen stations

**Note for Breathedge 1 veterans**: in the first game repaired stations gave unlimited refills. Breathedge 2 reworked the oxygen system — don't assume free infinite refills here.

## Alternative oxygen refills

- **Purified Oxygen**: Oxyworm x2 + Metal x1 + Ice x1 → +120 Oxygen per craft (base suit holds 600)
- [Oxygen guide](/en/guide/recipe-purified-oxygen)

## Reward

No stat or achievement reward — the payoff is a permanent oxygen refill point in the area. The station also comes back into play later: the final "activate the device" step of the **Chuck's Taxi** quest happens back at this location.

## FAQ

### 1. Where is the Public Oxygen Station in Breathedge 2?
Left of the train platform, opposite the Attendant platform, in the Station Outskirts.

### 2. What materials do I need to repair it?
Metal x3, Electronics x2, Wires x1, Electrical Tape x3.

### 3. I have the materials but can't repair it.
Restore the train's power and raise the pantograph first — the area thaws and the station becomes interactive. If it still doesn't work, advance the main quest.

### 4. How do I refill the oxygen station?
With Ice, the same way you refill the train. Don't turn all your ice into water.

### 5. Does repairing it give a reward?
No stat reward — it gives a permanent oxygen refill point, and it's used again at the end of Chuck's Taxi.
