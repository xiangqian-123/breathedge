## Quick answer

**Alkaline Solution** is **crafted from Alkali** at the crafting menu. It's the liquid that fills the **shuttle** in the Signal Jammer quest and the **Spraytron's** charge. Exact recipe amounts aren't confirmed yet — read the in-game recipe panel.

## What it is

Alkaline Solution is the liquid the Signal Jammer quest asks you to fill the shuttle with. It is *not* collected directly — you craft it from **Alkali**.

## How to make it

1. **Get Alkali** — extract from Alkaline Mole burrows with the Spraytron.
2. **Craft Alkaline Solution** at the crafting menu from Alkali.
