Breathedge 2 is an open world with a main storyline — follow the main quests and grab side quests along the way. Chapter 1 revolves around the train station area. Here is the main quest order.

## Main quest order

1. **Wake up**: after the crash, eat an apple and refill oxygen in the ship.
2. **Craft the Crowbar** from Metal to break locks (help Babe open cabinets/doors).
3. **Craft the Cutter**: Plastic + Aluminum + Metal.
4. **Craft the Thumper**: Metal + Plastic, to mine aluminum ore on asteroids.
5. **Fix the Kettle and Nutri-Pack-O-Mat**: produce water and food.
6. **Fix the oxygen station** so you don't have to return to the ship to breathe.
7. **Help Babe**: gather Duct Tape, Electrical Tape, and Glass to fix the window and heater.
8. **Three Antennas quest**: find the main antenna and the rest, install batteries + Electronics.
9. **Restore power**: find batteries, connect the rooftop power, fix the Ticket Booth.
10. **Police station**: dodge the turret (don't move when it looks at you — red light / green light), cut cables, avoid robots.
11. **Craft the Wrench** back home to repair equipment.
12. **Raise ambient temperature**: break ice, fix the Heater (with Glass), install Light Bulbs, complete the Snowman quest.
13. **Craft the Backpack** with Titanium to expand exploration.

## Side quests & collectibles

- **Attendant**: repair the damaged robot near the train station by returning her missing body parts for a Physical Protection bonus.
- **Collectibles**: toys, posters and other items left by other survivors; most achievements come from main quests or random encounters and grant stat bonuses.

## Walkthrough tips

- The colder it gets, the faster hunger drains — stock food before entering cold areas, or raise the temperature first.
- The **turret** works like red light / green light: stop moving while it faces you, move when it turns away.

> ⚠️ Early Access currently only includes Chapter 1; later chapters arrive with updates. This guide reflects the current build.
