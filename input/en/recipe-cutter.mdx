## Quick answer

The recipe is unlocked from the start, but you can't craft it right away — **Aluminum is mined with the [Thumper](/en/guide/recipe-thumper) first**. Recipe: **Aluminum x4, Plastic x2, Metal x4**.

## Recipe

| Material | Amount | How to get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aluminum | x4 | Mined from asteroid ore with the Thumper |
| Plastic | x2 | Dismantle wreckage |
| Metal | x4 | Dismantle wreckage |

## How to unlock

The recipe is unlocked from the start, but crafting is gated by Aluminum. The tool order is:

1. **Scrap** (Metal x2) — craftable from the start
2. **Thumper** (Metal x4 + Plastic x2) — craftable from the start
3. Mine **Aluminum** from asteroid ore with the Thumper
4. **Cutter** (Aluminum x4 + Plastic x2 + Metal x4)

## What it's for

Cuts objects and harvests certain exclusive resources (e.g. Electrical Tape on the red pipes).
