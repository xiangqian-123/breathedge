## Quick answer

Start at the **Chuck graffiti billboard** near the last antenna/memorial — a multi-step taxi repair (generator, radiator, solar panel, letters C/R/L) that ends with the **Alphabet achievement** + Thirst Drain Rate -5%.

## Location

The Chuck graffiti billboard near the last antenna/memorial.

## Materials needed

[Battery](/en/guide/material-battery), Battery cell x2, [Aluminum](/en/guide/material-aluminum), [Wires](/en/guide/material-wires), [Electronics](/en/guide/material-electronics), [Refined Metal](/en/guide/material-refined-metal), [Electrical Tape](/en/guide/material-electrical-tape), Techbits, [Titanium](/en/guide/material-titanium), [Nutriworm](/en/guide/material-nutriworm), [Ice](/en/guide/material-ice), letters C/R/L (see [Alphabet achievement](/en/guide/achievement-alphabet)).

## Steps

1. Inspect the billboard to start
2. Break the pump ice with [Scrap](/en/guide/recipe-scrap) under the platform
3. Repair the generator: battery + cables
4. Repair the radiator (roof of central building)
5. Repair and deploy the solar panel hinge
6. Search the taxi (hood, trunk, glove boxes)
7. Return the sign letters C/R/L
8. Serve two [homemade alcohol](/en/guide/recipe-alcohol) drinks to the robots
9. Insert the device, activate, fly in to find Chuck

## Patch notes (0.8.10 / 0.8.11)

- **0.8.10**: fixed getting **stuck in the taxi niche** while searching the taxi.
- **0.8.11**: you can no longer **toggle the same part on and off repeatedly** to complete the taxi inspection — the step now requires the real interaction.

## Reward

Alphabet achievement + Thirst Drain Rate -5%.
