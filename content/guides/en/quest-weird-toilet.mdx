## Quick answer

Open the **strange toilet** near the 10G antenna/memorial and Chuck's Taxi station (battery + panel + wiring repairs) to earn the **Weird Toilet achievement** + Cold Protection +10.

## Location

The strange toilet near the 10G antenna/memorial and Chuck's Taxi station.

## Materials needed

[Battery](/en/guide/material-battery); Panel: [Rubber](/en/guide/material-rubber) x2, Techbits x2, [Aluminum](/en/guide/material-aluminum) x4; Wiring: [Plastic](/en/guide/material-plastic) x2, [Refined Metal](/en/guide/material-refined-metal) x2, [Electronics](/en/guide/material-electronics) x2.

## Steps

1. Break the door beams with the Scrap tool
2. Place the battery in the slot
3. Repair both sides: Panel and Wiring
4. Open the door for the seat collectible, press the red button

> Patch 0.8.10: fixed getting **stuck after the back-door displacement**. If the door won't open or the quest stalls, update the game.

## Reward

Weird toilet achievement + Cold Protection +10.
