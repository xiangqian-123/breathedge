Survival is the core of Breathedge 2. The developers define it as "a story-driven survival adventure with a comedic tone," where survival, exploration, and crafting interlock. Here is how each survival system works.

## The four survival stats

| Stat | How to refill | Notes |
|------|------|------|
| **Oxygen** | Ship (auto) / oxygen station / Oxyworm / oxygen tank | Drains outside; Ice is the oxygen machine's raw material |
| **Hunger** | Nutri-Pack-O-Mat / raw food | Nutri-Pack ≈ 25, raw food ≈ 6 |
| **Thirst** | Kettle / water packs / chewing ice | Ice is also your oxygen source — don't waste it |
| **Rest** | Sleep in the ship | Sleeping costs nothing |

## Ambient temperature system

The world has an **ambient temperature** that drains your condition in cold areas:

- The colder it gets, the **faster hunger drains** — stock food before entering cold areas.
- Solutions: **break ice**, **repair the Heater** (with Glass), **install Light Bulbs**, and complete the **Snowman** quest to raise the temperature.
- Wear matching **Protective** gear to resist cold, combat damage, or radiation.

## Buffs and debuffs

The official buff/debuff system can stack, evolve, and last over time:

- **Eat well, sleep well, stay warm** → bonuses like increased stamina or reduced resource drain on your next outing.
- **Rotten apples, sleep deprivation** → penalties ranging from illness to impaired vision.

It rewards smart decisions and punishes reckless ones, without turning the game into hardcore survival.

## The Suit's grabbing arm

Your companion AI **Suit** now returns with an **upgradable mechanical grabbing arm** — it moves heavy objects, rips off panels, grabs distant items, and holds drifting objects steady while you smash them.

## Survival quick reference

| Input | Condition | Result |
|------|------|------|
| Chew ice | No clean water | Quench thirst + consume oxygen material |
| Eat rotten apple | No cooked food | Delay hunger + possible poisoning |
| Sleep & stay warm | Regular routine | Bonuses next outing |
| Sleep deprivation | Long-term | Illness / impaired vision |

> ⚠️ Early Access mechanics may change with updates — check in-game.
