## Quick answer

Rubber comes from **dark rubber pads and panels attached to industrial structures** — most of them are frozen in ice at first. **Repair Service Warmers to melt the ice**, then **cut the exposed pads with the Cutter**.

## Where to find rubber

### Location 1 — The frozen structure past the Pornhubble antenna

The most reliable early source is a large ice-covered structure you reach by flying **past the Pornhubble antenna and keeping going**. It stands out from the scattered wreckage around it.

1. Fly to the Pornhubble antenna
2. Continue past it toward the large frozen structure
3. Melt the ice (see below), then cut the rubber pads

### Location 2 — Lower Depot industrial area

Once you reach the **Depot**, industrial equipment on its lower levels is covered in rubber pads and panels. From this point on, rubber stops being scarce.

### Location 3 — Yellow tunnel wreckage

Wreckage surfaces along the large yellow tunnel and in the stretch past it (before the spooky station) also carry rubber pads. This is the spot multiple guides point early players to.

## What rubber looks like

Rubber reads as **dark rubberized pads and panels stuck to machinery** — a flat black pad once the ice is gone, closer to matting than to armor plating or ore. It is never a loose chunk floating in space.

Don't go looking for black rocks. If the dark thing is shaped like a panel or a pad, that's rubber.

## How to collect rubber

1. **Thaw it first.** Repair nearby **Service Warmers** (heaters). Each working warmer melts ice in its area and exposes the rubber panels underneath.
2. **Cut with the Cutter.** Walk up to an exposed pad and use the **Cutter** — the interaction prompt reads **Cut**.

No Cutter yet? The recipe is **4 Aluminum + 2 Plastic + 4 Metal**, unlocked from the start. But it's not craftable right away — **Aluminum is drilled out of asteroid ore with the [Thumper](/en/guide/recipe-thumper) first**, so the tool order is Scrap → Thumper → mine Aluminum → Cutter.

## Can't pick up rubber?

- **Wrong tool.** Rubber pads only respond to the **Cutter**. Scrap — the same tool as the Crowbar, just a different name — won't harvest them, and Breathedge 2 has no shears (the Breathedge 1 tool doesn't exist here).
- **Still frozen.** If the pad is inside ice, no tool works. Find and repair the local Service Warmer first.
- **Bonus drop.** Smashing old suitcases can also drop rubber as a container bonus, but the pads are the dependable source.

## What rubber is used for

| Use | Rubber needed |
| --- | --- |
| Gas Tank (full recipe: Wires x4 + Refined Metal x2 + Rubber x2 + Aluminum x4) | x2 |
| Engine repair (main story) | ~4 per engine (sources range 2-4; check the in-game prompt) |
| Shuttle hull repair (main story) | x4 |

> ⚠️ Recipe quantities are from the current Early Access build and may change with updates.

## Does rubber respawn?

Respawn behavior isn't documented for the current build — don't wait around. Instead, **repair more Service Warmers**: every new warmer melts more ice and exposes more pads. Progressing through the Depot also opens up location 2.

## Other materials nearby

While sweeping the wreckage and structures, grab what spawns alongside:

- [Metal](/en/guide/material-metal) — floating scrap, metal spheres, barrels, crates
- [Wires](/en/guide/material-wires) — electrical panels, exposed cables (Cutter)
- [Plastic](/en/guide/material-plastic) — station equipment, broken parts (Scrap)
- [Electronics](/en/guide/material-electronics) — appliances like washing machines (dismantle)
- [Light Bulb](/en/guide/material-light-bulb) — lamp fittings with an Unscrew prompt
- Electrical tape — taped pipes and machinery (Cutter)

## FAQ

### 1. Where is Rubber in Breathedge 2?
Dark rubber pads on industrial structures. Start at the frozen structure past the Pornhubble antenna, then the yellow tunnel wreckage; the lower Depot has plenty.

### 2. Why can't I pick up Rubber?
Either you're not using the Cutter, or the pad is still frozen in ice. Repair the local Service Warmer to thaw it.

### 3. Does Rubber respawn?
Not documented in Early Access. Repair more Service Warmers to melt more ice and expose more pads instead of waiting.

### 4. How much Rubber do I need?
Plan for at least 6 early: 2 for the Gas Tank and about 4 for the engine repair.

### 5. Can Scrap collect Rubber?
No. Scrap (the Crowbar) won't cut rubber pads — use the Cutter.
