## Quick answer

The Ticket Booth is **left of the Tickets building, next to the turret**. Repair the sparking side with **Metal x3 + Electrical Tape x2 + Electronics x1**, then solve the knob puzzle: **first down, second up, third right** — a Token drops out. Repair the **back office console** (Metal x4 + Electrical Tape x1 + Wires x2 + Electronics x2) to disable the turret permanently.

## Location

The Ticket Booth sits to the **left of the Tickets station building**. Find the turret first, then go around its left side.

## Dealing with the turret

The turret is **motion-sensing**: it turns **red** right before firing. **Stand still while it's red** and it won't shoot.

The main story's antenna quest only **weakens** the turret — cut the two yellow cables at the worn spots with the Cutter, pull out the four cooling units/batteries, and smash the top control panel with the Scrap. It can still turn and shoot after that. The only way to shut it down **for good** is the back office console below.

> This is an optional side quest — you can complete it whenever you want without blocking the main story.

## Repair materials

The **sparking side** of the booth needs:

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Metal | x3 |
| Electrical Tape | x2 |
| Electronics | x1 |

## Knob puzzle answer

Hint: the back of the Tickets building has the word **"Racket"** with arrows. At the front of the booth, twist the knobs:

1. **First knob: down**
2. **Second knob: up**
3. **Third knob: right**

A **Token** drops out of the machine when done correctly. Community guides say the Token is used at the vending machines on the main platform — the exact use isn't fully documented.

## Back office — permanent turret shutdown

Go around the **outside of the building to the rear** and open the rear door (not the front). Repair the console inside:

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Metal | x4 |
| Electrical Tape | x1 |
| Wires | x2 |
| Electronics | x2 |

This **disables the turret for good**.

## Rewards

- The vending machines on the **main platform become usable**, selling ice, food and water
- The turret is permanently disabled, making later exploration safer

## FAQ

### 1. Where is the Ticket Booth in Breathedge 2?
Left of the Tickets building, next to the turret — circle around the turret's left side to reach it.

### 2. I repaired the booth but the vending machines don't work.
Solve the knob puzzle (down, up, right) — repairing alone isn't enough, the Token comes from the puzzle.

### 3. The turret still shoots after the main quest.
The main quest only slows the turret down. Permanent shutdown requires repairing the console in the back office.

### 4. How do I get past the turret?
Watch for it turning red before firing — stand still while it's red, move while it's not. Then reach the booth from the left.

### 5. Where is the back office entrance?
Around the outside of the building, at the rear — the rear door, not the front entrance.
