## Quick answer

The opening area and main-story hub. Your ship refills oxygen here, and the early quests revolve around it.

## Quests here

Wake up, fix the Kettle, fix the Oxygen Station, help Babe, Public Oxygen Station

## Materials here

Metal, Plastic (starting-area wreckage)

## Achievements here

Emergency brake, Hacker, Click-click

## Danger

None (safe zone)
