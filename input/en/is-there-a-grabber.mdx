## Quick answer

**No.** Breathedge 2's Early Access build has **no Grabber tool**. Complete tool lists for the game contain only **Scrap (Crowbar), Thumper, Cutter, Twister, Spraytron and Flashlight** — no Grabber in any recipe table.

## Where the confusion comes from

The **first Breathedge** had a Grabber — used to safely pick up Glass and to extract Resin and Alkali from veins, crafted at the Processor. That's where the search results come from.

Guides that mention a Breathedge 2 "Grabber" are usually recycling Breathedge 1 info, or describing the demo build. Treat any Grabber recipe you find as **Breathedge 1 content**.

## What about Glass in Breathedge 2?

In Breathedge 2, glass shards are found by **exploring wreckage and picking them up** — not extracted with a tool. The destroyed rescue shuttle wreck (near the cooling gel stream, right of the starting shuttle) is one reported spot. If a guide tells you to equip a Grabber for glass, it's describing Breathedge 1.

## FAQ

### 1. Is there a Grabber in Breathedge 2?
No. The current Early Access tool list has no Grabber.

### 2. Why do guides mention a Breathedge Grabber?
They're talking about Breathedge 1, which had a Grabber for glass and resin — or about the old demo.

### 3. How do I collect Glass in Breathedge 2?
Explore wreckage and pick glass shards up by hand — no tool extraction.
