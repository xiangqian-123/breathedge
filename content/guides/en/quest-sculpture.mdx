## Quick answer

The platform with three toilets (fly up to the left of Hubble).

## Location

The platform with three toilets (fly up to the left of Hubble).

## Materials needed

Scrap tool (break yellow ice for clay).

## Steps

1. Find the clay sculpture
2. Collect 3 clay pieces (beside the sculpture, inside the first port-a-potty, under the platform by breaking yellow ice)
3. Bring them back, attach, and sculpt

## Reward

Go down in history achievement + Maximum Satiety +10.
