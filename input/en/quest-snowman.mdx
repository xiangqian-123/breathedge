## Quick answer

The incomplete Snowman sits **near the Foreman, on the way to the 10G antenna** (through the yellow tunnel, around grid BB 30 near the Mustache Robot). Collect all **5 parts** — Carrot, Cigarette, Beard, Left Arm, Hat — and assemble them for **Cold Protection +5** and the **Now That's a Snowman!** achievement.

## Before you start

The Snowman only becomes interactable after the main story reaches the **"raise the ambient temperature"** objective. If the area is still frozen and parts give no prompt, advance the main quest first.

The Snowman sits next to the **Leader's Statue** side quest — do both in one trip.

## All 5 snowman parts

| Part | Where to find it | Attach to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cigarette | With the Carrot (see below) — install it first | mouth |
| Carrot | With the Cigarette — install it second | nose |
| Beard | Past the Santa corpse — looks like a plain white snowball, easy to miss | chin |
| Left Arm | Right side just outside the tunnel exit (heading west) | shoulder |
| Hat | Sharp left after the tunnel, toward the Leader's Statue, near a lit billboard | head |

### Carrot & Cigarette

The pair spawns together. Go **through the yellow tunnel, turn left**, and find the billboard about "smoking and betrayal" — the carrot and cigarette float in front of a sign nearby. Some guides point to the same spot as "on the sign near the local Service Warmer".

**They are frozen in ice** — repair the gas lamp / **Service Warmer** in that area to melt them first.

Install the **Cigarette first, then the Carrot**.

### Beard (Mustache)

From the Snowman, fly toward the **Santa-Claus corpse**, pass it, then turn left. The beard looks like an ordinary white snowball — search for the "beard" interaction prompt rather than a recognizable shape. Attach to the chin.

### Left Arm

**Right side just outside the tunnel exit** (heading west out of the tunnel). Some players describe the arms as two snowballs floating around the Snowman platform. Attach to the shoulder.

### Hat

Go through the tunnel again, take a **sharp left**, and circle toward the **Leader's Statue**. The hat floats near a lit billboard (another guide: near the corpse with its tongue stuck to the poster). Attach to the head.

## Heater note

The in-game term is **Service Warmer**, not "heater". If a part gives no pickup prompt, the local Service Warmer isn't repaired yet — or the main-story temperature step hasn't been reached. Check your journal: some guides say the quest log itself asks for Cold Protection 100, which you raise at the Space Suit wardrobe on the train.

## Reward

- **Now That's a Snowman!** achievement
- **Cold Protection +5** (permanent)

## FAQ

### 1. Where is the Snowman in Breathedge 2?
Near the Foreman, on the way to the 10G antenna — go through the yellow tunnel; it's around grid BB 30 near the Mustache Robot.

### 2. Why can't I pick up the Carrot and Cigarette?
They're frozen in ice. Repair the Service Warmer (gas lamp) in that area to melt them, and make sure the main story has reached the temperature-raising objective.

### 3. Which part goes where?
Cigarette to the mouth, Carrot to the nose, Beard to the chin, Left Arm to the shoulder, Hat to the head.

### 4. I can't find the Beard.
It looks like a plain white snowball past the Santa corpse — look for the interaction prompt, not a beard shape.

### 5. What's the Snowman reward?
Cold Protection +5 and the Now That's a Snowman! achievement.
