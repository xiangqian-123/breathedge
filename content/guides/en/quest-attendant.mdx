## Quick answer

Find the broken **attendant robot** on the platform left of the station (frozen until power is restored), reassemble its head, torso and arm to earn the **Attendant achievement** and Physical Protection +2.

## Location

The attendant platform near the station, to the left (may be frozen before power is restored).

## Materials needed

None (find the body parts).

## Steps

1. Find the attendant platform — the robot is missing head, torso and arm
2. Head: floating near the [oxygen station](/en/guide/quest-public-oxygen-station) to the left
3. Torso: underneath the rocks below the platform
4. Arm: floating above the yellow/white bridge structures to the right
5. Place the pieces on the base to reassemble

> Patch 0.8.10: fixed a bug where the **Station Caretaker could get stuck and be moved out while installing parts**. If reassembling freezes or the robot won't accept a part, you're on an old build — update the game.

## Reward

Attendant achievement + Physical Protection +2.
