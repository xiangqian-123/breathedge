## Quick answer

The rocket toilet along the train tracks (right side).

## Location

The rocket toilet along the train tracks (right side).

## Materials needed

Plastic x3, Metal x4, Wires x2.

## Steps

1. Find the rocket toilet along the tracks
2. Repair with the materials
3. Approach and hold E to test the engines

## Reward

Shipbuilder achievement + Oxygen Consumption -5%.
