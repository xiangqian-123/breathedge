## Quick answer

On patch 0.8.11, the reflector **petals count once they're at least 80% open**. If your journal still names **ice** or **hinge** repair, finish that stage first — don't keep re-opening petals.

## When & where

The 10G Antenna is reached **after the Depot's Service Warmers**, on the Chapter 1 communications route. It's the step right before you start hunting the Signal Jammer.

## The repair route

The quest runs in roughly this order:

1. **Clear ice / cables** — if this step won't register, update the game (the non-counting bug was fixed in 0.8.6)
2. **Repair the hinge** — open the hinge panel to read the exact material list
3. **Unfold the reflector petals** — each required petal needs to be ≥80% open
4. **Install the battery**
5. **Jammer locator** — completing the antenna gives you the jammer locator sequence

## The 80% petal rule (0.8.11)

Petals count toward the objective once they're **80% open** — measured **per petal**, not as a whole-quest percentage. Open each required petal once and move on; don't keep wiggling a petal that's already counted.

## Hinge materials (0.8.11 fix)

Patch 0.8.11 corrected the hinge's **material display**. Open the hinge panel and read each item and amount **live** — older screenshots may show the wrong list.

> The hinge shares materials with other antenna, train, and Service Warmer repairs — don't dump your spare parts elsewhere first.

## Cold Protection ≠ Ambient Temperature

If a journal goal mentions temperature, it's the **Depot's Ambient Temperature** (raised by Service Warmers), not your suit's **Cold Protection**. They're separate systems.

## Not working? (FAQ)

### Petals aren't counting
Each petal must be ≥80% open. If one still won't count, check whether the journal actually still lists **ice** or **hinge** — finish that stage first.

### The antenna won't unfold
Finish the current objective first. If it's a hinge or ice step, complete it before touching petals.

### I can't find the materials
Open the hinge panel for the corrected list — don't rely on an old screenshot.

## Next: Signal Jammer

Completing the 10G Antenna starts the **Signal Jammer** search — three strong jamming readings you triangulate to find the facility.
