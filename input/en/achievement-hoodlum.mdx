## Quick answer

Unscrew 10 light bulbs while exploring. The total is cumulative, so they do not need to be collected in the same area.

## Official description

> I don't know how many people it takes to screw in a light bulb, but it only takes one man to unscrew it.

## Reward

Unlocking it permanently grants **Heat Protection +5**. Achievement bonuses stack, and their effects can be checked on the in-game Rewards screen.
