## Quick answer

**Rubber** comes from **industrial wreckage along the yellow tunnel** — dark industrial pads and panels, cut with the Cutter. Smashing old suitcases may also drop it.

## Where to find it

Several sources point to similar areas (exact descriptions differ slightly — check in-game):

- Wreckage surfaces along the large yellow tunnel
- The area past the tunnel, before the spooky station
- Dark industrial pads and panels, harvested with Cut
- Breaking old suitcases may drop some

## How to get it

Cut industrial pads / panels with the Cutter.

## What it's used for

- **Gas Tank**: Rubber x2
- **Engine repair**: about 4 units (main-story related)
