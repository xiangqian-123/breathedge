## Quick answer

Repair and set up the **three communication antennas** in the Station Outskirts — the **Government Probe**, the **Homemade Beacon**, and the **Attendant Bridge**. Finishing all three unlocks the **Twister** and gives you **new coordinates** that send you to the Tickets Building.

## When this quest starts

This is an early Chapter 1 main quest, after you fix the Kettle and the Public Oxygen Station. It's what gates your way into the Tickets Building. Bring a **Cutter** and a **Battery** before heading out.

## The three antennas

| # | Antenna | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Government Probe | Repair the probe |
| 2 | Homemade Beacon | Set up the Tuning Unit + Battery |
| 3 | Attendant Bridge | Locate the antenna parts |

These three landmarks sit around the Station Outskirts. They're communication structures — a different objective from the single broken antenna in the "Repair the Antenna" side quest.

## Materials & tools

- **Cutter** — to reach and cut access to the parts
- **Battery** — to power the Homemade Beacon
- **Antenna Part** — found near the structures
- **Tuning Unit** — set up at the Homemade Beacon

> Community walkthroughs name slightly different items between builds — if your journal lists a different part, follow the journal's objective text.

## Steps

1. Find the **Government Probe** and repair it
2. Head to the **Homemade Beacon** and set up the **Tuning Unit + Battery**
3. Locate the **antenna parts** near one of the structures
4. Complete the setup to receive **new coordinates** and the **Twister unlock**

> Patch 0.8.10 fixed a bug that could get you **stuck while installing antenna parts**. If the install won't progress, update the game.

## Reward

- **Twister unlock** — a tool you'll need later in the Depot and beyond
- **New coordinates** — pointing to the next main quest

## Next: Get the Service Routes

The new coordinates lead to the **Tickets Building**, where you fix the power and the **Ticket Booth**. After that the story continues toward the **Depot** and the **Service Warmers**.

## FAQ

### Where are the three antennas?
Station Outskirts — the Government Probe, the Homemade Beacon, and the Attendant Bridge.

### What do I need?
Cutter, Battery, Antenna Part, and Tuning Unit.

### I'm stuck while installing the antenna parts.
Update to 0.8.10 or later — that patch fixed the install freeze.

### What does this quest unlock?
The **Twister** tool, plus new coordinates to the Tickets Building.
