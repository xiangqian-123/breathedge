## Quick answer

Early Access includes the **Chapter 1 story**, a large explorable area, and the core survival/crafting/upgrade/combat systems. EA is expected to last **16-20 months**; 1.0 is undated.

## What's included

- Chapter 1 main story
- A large explorable area (around the station)
- Survival, crafting, upgrade, and combat systems
- Main + side quests
- 39 achievements

## When does 1.0 release?

**Undated.** The developers expect Early Access to last roughly **16-20 months**, adjustable by development progress and player feedback. 1.0 arrives when the game is close to the full experience.

## Will the price go up during EA?

Yes. The developers state the price will **gradually increase** with content updates, and the full release will cost more.
