## Quick answer

Repair three broken engines near the **Foreman/Snowman** (toward the 10G antenna) to unlock the **Boosters recipe** (flight speed). Police appear when you fix the last one — run away.

## Location

The first broken engine near the Foreman/Snowman, toward the 10G antenna.

## Materials needed

Per engine: [Electrical Tape](/en/guide/material-electrical-tape) x2, [Aluminum](/en/guide/material-aluminum) x2, [Rubber](/en/guide/material-rubber) x2.

## Steps

1. Repair the small engines with the materials (three total)
2. Police appear when you fix the last one — run away

## Reward

Unlocks the Boosters equipment recipe (flight speed).
