## Gathering tips

- **Pick up at least one** of any new material — it can unlock a new recipe.
- **Plastic is essential**: Cutter, Thumper, Twister, Flashlight and Backpack all need it — break it on sight.
- **Titanium is scarce**: only from spiked debris — save 4 for the Backpack.
- **Harvesting is verb-driven** — Break, Cut, Unscrew, Pull, Drill — the right verb saves tool wear.

> ⚠️ Early Access resource distribution may change; locations are cross-verified but check in-game.
