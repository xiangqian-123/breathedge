## Quick answer

Break down debris (needs multiple hits); black clumps at cable ends; concentrated near the starting station.

## How to get it

Hit with the crowbar.

## What it's used for

Cutter, Thumper, Twister, Flashlight, Backpack. Essential — break it on sight.
