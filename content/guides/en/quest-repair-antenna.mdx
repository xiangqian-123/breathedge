## Quick answer

The broken antenna to the left of the train platform.

## Location

The broken antenna to the left of the train platform.

## Materials needed

None (just find the missing pieces).

## Steps

1. Find the broken antenna near the station
2. Find Antenna Debris 1: past the antenna, floating to the left
3. Find Antenna Debris 2: behind the platform, near an asteroid and ice chunk
4. Bring the pieces back to repair the antenna

## Reward

Repairs the antenna (main-story related).
