## Quick answer

Past the second train car and the 'everyone is dead here' sign, at the heart-sign bench platform.

## Location

Past the second train car and the 'everyone is dead here' sign, at the heart-sign bench platform.

## Materials needed

Scrap tool (break the love locks).

## Steps

1. Fly straight out to the heart-sign bench platform
2. Walk past the couple to the fence
3. Break all the love locks with the Scrap tool

## Reward

Traditional Values achievement + Maximum Health +10.
