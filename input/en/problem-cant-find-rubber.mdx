## Quick answer

**Rubber comes from dark industrial pads and panels, cut with the Cutter.** It's mostly on wreckage surfaces along the yellow tunnel; breaking old suitcases may also drop it.

## Steps

1. Make sure you have the Cutter (not the Scrap)
2. Head to the yellow tunnel area and look for dark industrial pads/panels
3. Cut them with the Cutter
