Breathedge 2's Early Access build has **39 achievements**. Unlike normal completion badges, each achievement **permanently grants a stat bonus** (oxygen, health, protection, speed, and more), and these bonuses stack. You can check their effects on the in-game Rewards screen.

Click any achievement for a dedicated unlock guide (quick answer, official description, and stat reward).
