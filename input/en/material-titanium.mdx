## Quick answer

Titanium comes from **small spiky debris balls floating in open space** — smash them with the **Scrap** tool. Your first reliable finds come **from the Depot stage onward**; if you have zero early on, it's a story-progress gate, not bad luck.

## What titanium looks like

A **compact, spiked or bristled debris piece floating in space** — a small spiky ball. Some players describe it as a blue metal sphere covered in spikes.

Don't assume every gray rock is titanium, and don't trust "asteroid vein" descriptions — the consistent shape across guides is the **floating spiky ball**. Remember the shape, not coordinates: exact placements change between patches.

## First titanium locations

### From the Depot stage onward

Titanium becomes dependable once you reach the **Depot**: sweep the area around it and along the main-story path. Use working **Service Warmers** and completed objectives as return landmarks, and search outward from them.

### Yellow tunnel stretch

Some players report titanium balls in the stretch **after the large yellow tunnel, before the spooky station** — a good early sweep if you're passing through anyway.

## Required tool

**Scrap** — the same tool as the Crowbar, just a different name. Smash the spiky balls and pick up the titanium. If the in-game prompt differs from this guide, follow the in-game prompt (tool interactions have changed across patches).

## Old Demo route warning

The old Demo FAQ pointed players **behind Chuck's Taxi** — **do not farm there**. That was a Demo-era location and isn't a reliable Early Access route; patch 0.8.7 reportedly moved some Taxi-area collectibles. Treat Demo guides and Breathedge 1 guides as history, not as current farming spots.

## What titanium is used for

| Use | Titanium needed |
| --- | --- |
| **Backpack** (full recipe: Titanium x4 + Aluminum x4 + Plastic x2 + Electric Tape x4) | x4 |
| Shuttle hull repair (main story, with Metal x4 + Duct Tape x1 + Rubber x4) | x4 |

The **Backpack** raises carry weight — the single best reason to hunt titanium (one player report cites **110 → 185**, but the exact capacity is unconfirmed). Its blueprint unlocks when you obtain the **Painted Corpse**.

**Worth the detour if** you're 1-2 short of the Backpack. **Not worth stopping everything** if you have none — keep pushing the main story and they'll appear.

## Can't find titanium?

1. **Advance the main story.** Early titanium scarcity is a progress gate. If the current area shows no titanium at all, the answer is usually "keep going", not "your save is broken".
2. **Don't smash unrelated objects hoping for a drop.** Note the area name + your current objective + the tooltip text, and move on.
3. **Check your guide's date.** Recipes and placements change between patches — the in-game prompt and crafting panel are the final word.

> ⚠️ Early Access values may change with updates. Respawn behavior is not documented — treat each ball as a one-time pickup.

## FAQ

### 1. Where is Titanium in Breathedge 2?
Small spiky balls floating in space — first dependable amounts at the Depot stage, with some reported in the yellow tunnel stretch.

### 2. What tool mines Titanium?
Scrap (the Crowbar). Smash the spiky balls.

### 3. Why can't I find Titanium early?
You're probably not supposed to yet. Titanium opens up with the Depot stage — advance the main story.

### 4. Should I use the old Demo route behind Chuck's Taxi?
No. That's a Demo-era location and isn't a reliable EA farming spot.

### 5. What is Titanium used for?
The Backpack (x4, raises carry weight) and the main-story shuttle hull repair (x4).
