## Quick answer

The Ticket Booth is **left of the Tickets building, next to the turret**. Repair: **Metal x3 + Electrical Tape x2 + Electronics x1**. Knob puzzle: **first down, second up, third right** — a Token drops out when done correctly.

## Location

The Ticket Booth sits to the left of the Tickets station building. Find the turret first, then go around its left side to reach the booth.

## How to get there (dealing with the turret)

During Chapter 1 main quests, slow the turret first — **cut the power cable and break the power panel**. Once it's slowed, circle to the left side to safely reach the booth.

## Repair materials

The sparking side of the booth needs:

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Metal | x3 |
| Electrical Tape | x2 |
| Electronics | x1 |

## Knob puzzle answer

Hint: the back of the Tickets building has the word **"Racket"** with arrows. At the front of the booth, twist the knobs:

1. **First knob: down**
2. **Second knob: up**
3. **Third knob: right**

A **Token** drops out of the machine when done correctly.

## Back office

Go around to the back of the building and open the door. Repair the console inside:

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Metal | x4 |
| Electrical Tape | x1 |
| Wires | x2 |
| Electronics | x2 |

This **disables the turret for good**.

## Rewards

- Vending machines appear on the main platform, giving ice, food and water
- The turret is permanently disabled, making later exploration safer
