## DIY module upgrade system

The core of upgrades is the official **DIY (Do It Yourself) system**:

- Basic tools break down into **seven module categories**: Sharp, Impact, Utility, Protective, Active, Capacity, Biological.
- Modules have **rarity and quality**; the same module category works across multiple devices and even repairs world objects.
- **Removable**: modules can be swapped freely between tools until they break, but they **can't be duplicated**.
- **Recyclable when broken**: broken DIY modules disappear, but you can recycle your favorites using basic resources; **basic tools are always kept**.
- **Protective gear**: Protective modules are armor; different armor favors cold/combat/radiation, a full matching set grants a bonus, and armor wears out over time.

→ [See exact tool/station upgrade values](/en/guide/recipe-upgrades)

> The game's survival motto: "It doesn't matter where your hands grow from, as long as they're golden." The DIY system turns gathering from "hoarding numbers" into "solving problems".

## Material priority tips

- **Plastic is essential**: Cutter, Thumper, Twister, Flashlight and Backpack all need it — break it down on sight.
- **Aluminum** matters for Cutter, Twister, Gas Tank and Backpack.
- **Titanium is scarce**: barely used in early recipes — save 4 for the Backpack.

> ⚠️ Early Access recipes may change with updates; check in-game for final values.
