## Quick answer

After the 10G Antenna, follow **three strong jamming readings** to triangulate the **Jammer Facility**. Inside, restore power, work the airlock, fill the **shuttle** with **Alkaline Solution**, then **clean it with the Spraytron** before retrieving the jammer.

## When this quest starts

It starts after the **10G Antenna** is repaired. The journal has to confirm the Depot's Ambient Temperature first — flying into colder structures early won't trigger it. Bring a **Crowbar** and spare oxygen.

## The three readings

The readings are **directional** — they don't have fixed names:

1. **Reading #1** — starts the triangulation
2. **Reading #2** — narrows the direction
3. **Reading #3** — reveals the structure the signal converges on

Follow the interference signal to each point, and let each scan or dialogue finish before moving on.

## Find the Jammer Facility

After the third reading the journal tracks **"Find the Signal Jammer"**. Entry is objective-gated — restore the **external power** and work the access sequence first (there's no code to find outside).

## Power / airlock progression

Inside, the route runs one device at a time. The cooling system's pump-out sequence locks the airlock as part of the path, then the journal adds a manual reactivation step.

## Fill the shuttle

The order is: find the jammer → **fill the shuttle with Alkaline Solution** → **clean the shuttle with the Spraytron** → close the lid → activate the shuttle → retrieve the jammer. The tank only opens once the filling objective appears — similar-looking items won't count.

## How to get Alkaline Solution

**Alkaline Solution is crafted from Alkali**, not collected directly. **Alkali** is extracted from **Alkaline Mole burrows** with the **Spraytron** (yield 3-5 per deposit); craft the solution from it, then use it to fill the shuttle. The Spraytron's own role at the shuttle is to **clean** it — a separate step from filling. The game tracks the exact amounts in your objective — follow that prompt for the current numbers.

## Retrieve the Jammer

After the shuttle sequence, follow the parasite encounter to the retrieve objective. Take the jammer only when its prompt appears, then use the **Crowbar** once the circular indicator stops and turns green.

## Jammer dialogue / progression stuck (0.8.11)

Patch 0.8.11 fixed a bug where **dying during active dialogue** could block progression, plus a jammer dialogue audio issue. If the jammer won't progress, update the game.

## FAQ

### "Problem filling the shuttle"
The tank opens only when the filling objective is active — make sure you have **crafted Alkaline Solution** (not a similar item) and that the journal shows the fill objective. The Spraytron **cleans** the shuttle in a separate step; it doesn't produce the solution.

### I can't find the readings
They're directional — follow the interference signal, and stop at each interactable point to let the scan finish.

### The jammer won't trigger
Make sure the Depot's Ambient Temperature is confirmed complete first, then update to 0.8.11.
