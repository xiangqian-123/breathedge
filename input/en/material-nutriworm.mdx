## Quick answer

**Nutriworms float near the water tower / frozen green nutrient tank at Chuck's Taxi** — fat green worms hovering in one specific spot. Grab them while you're doing the Chuck's Taxi quest. They're the ingredient for **Alcohol** (Nutriworm x1 + Ice x2).

## Where to find nutriworms

Fly to the **Chuck's Taxi platform** and look for:

- The **water tower structure to the left of the taxi platform** — the worms hover above/near it
- Some guides describe the same landmark as a **frozen, broken green nutrient tank above Chuck's Taxi**

Both point to the same structure. The worms float **out in the open** — they're not hidden inside anything. About **8-12** spawn there per trip (from player reports; not yet confirmed by more sources).

> 🐛 **Map-label bug**: the spawn point is labeled "Oxyworm gathering spot" on the map (likely a bug). Don't navigate by the map name — go by the landmark above. This bug is also why so many players "can't find" Nutriworms.

## What nutriworms look like

Fat **green worms** floating in one fixed spot — they hover rather than drift through open space like Oxyworms.

## How to use them: Alcohol recipe

Open the crafting menu (**C**) and craft:

| Recipe | Materials |
| --- | --- |
| Alcohol | Nutriworm x1 + Ice x2 |

Ice comes from frozen blocks and icy wreckage all over the map — the worms are the only part you have to travel for. The recipe unlocks after you obtain your first Alcohol.

**What Alcohol is for:**

- **Medkit**: Alcohol x1 + Fabric x2 — the most practical reason to stock up
- **Chuck's Taxi quest**: two drinks for the drunk robot at the end of the chain
- **Achievements**: drink 50 bottles total (Radiation Protection +10) and 10 in a row (Thirst Drain −5%)

## Nutriworm vs Oxyworm

Getting these two confused means searching the wrong place.

| | Nutriworm | Oxyworm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use | Alcohol only | Oxygen crafts: Purified Oxygen (Oxyworm x2 + Metal x1 + Ice x1 → +120 Oxygen) |
| Spawn | Fixed spot near Chuck's Taxi's water tower/tank | Open space, drifting |
| Related | Alcohol → Medkit / quests / achievements | Oxyworm Collector achievement at 300 collected (+30 max oxygen) |

Oxyworm Nests are a separate object (direct oxygen refill points, not a way to collect Oxyworms).

## Respawn

Not documented for the current build. Take as many as you need while you're at the platform.

## FAQ

### 1. Where is the Nutriworm in Breathedge 2?
Near the water tower structure / frozen green nutrient tank at Chuck's Taxi — fat green worms floating in one spot.

### 2. Nutriworm or Oxyworm — which is which?
Nutriworm = green, fixed spot at Chuck's Taxi, used only for Alcohol. Oxyworm = drifting in open space, used for oxygen crafts.

### 3. Why can't I find the spawn on the map?
Known bug: the map labels it "Oxyworm gathering spot". Go by the water tower / green tank landmark instead.

### 4. How do I make Alcohol?
Nutriworm x1 + Ice x2 in the crafting menu. The recipe unlocks after your first Alcohol.

### 5. Do Nutriworms respawn?
Not documented — collect what you need per visit.
