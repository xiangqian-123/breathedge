## Quick answer

Near the Foreman, on the way to the 10G antenna.

## Location

Near the Foreman, on the way to the 10G antenna.

## Materials needed

None (collect the parts).

## Steps

1. Find the incomplete snowman
2. Collect and assemble: Carrot & Cigarette (billboard past the yellow tunnel), Hat (sharp left then circle back to the Leader statue), Left Arm (right of the tunnel), Beard (past the Santa corpse)

## Reward

Now That's a Snowman! achievement + Cold Protection +5.
