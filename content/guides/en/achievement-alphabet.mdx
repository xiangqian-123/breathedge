## Quick answer

After repairing the antenna, find the missing **R, C, and L** letters and place them back onto the Chuck's Taxi sign. R floats on the opposite side of the platform, C floats in front of the sign, and L is under the drunks' platform (see locations below).

## Official description

> Mbyae the oderr of lterets isn't so imprnotat, as it semes, but it's btteer coerrct tahn not. Plrbbaoy.

*(The flavor text is intentionally misspelled — it reads "Maybe the order of letters isn't so important, as it seems, but it's better correct than not. Probably.")*

## Letter locations

The sign reads **"First Class"** before it's defaced; removing three letters turns it into "Fist Ass". Find all three and snap them back onto the sign:

| Letter | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| **C** | Floats directly **in front of the sign** — the easiest one; grab and attach it first. |
| **L** | Under the platform where two **drunks sit next to an umbrella** (east of the sign). Talk to them first — they ask for alcohol and may throw bottles — then check under the platform. |
| **R** | Floats **freely in space on the opposite side of the platform** — easy to miss against the blue background. |

Once all three are snapped in, the sign is repaired and the achievement unlocks.

## Demo vs Early Access (letters changed)

Official sources confirm the letter positions **changed between the Demo and the current Early Access build** — older Demo guides point to the wrong spots. Patch **0.8.10** also fixed the letter placement for the **Latin-American Spanish** version. Use the current-build locations above, not an old Demo walkthrough.

## Reward

Unlocking it permanently grants **Thirst Drain Rate -5%**. Achievement bonuses stack, and their effects can be checked on the in-game Rewards screen.
