## Quick answer

Refined Metal **cannot be crafted** — Metal and Refined Metal are **separate resources**. It comes from a handful of **fixed pickups**: below the antenna platform (1), in the **secret stash under the train station** (2, west side, open with the Twister), and in the **red panel on the giant wreck** near the double yellow pipes (1). Your first pickup unlocks the **Gas Tank** blueprint.

## Correction: "4 Metal → 1 Refined Metal" is wrong

Several guides repeat that smelting 4 Metal gives 1 Refined Metal. **No source actually confirms this** — it's a misreading of the Spraytron recipe, which *consumes* 4 Refined Metal. In-game, no amount of ordinary Metal replaces Refined Metal.

## Refined Metal locations

### 1. Below the antenna platform

The platform the antenna sits on has 1 Refined Metal **directly underneath it**.

### 2. Secret stash under the train station — 2 pieces

Approach the train station from the **west side**. The stash handle is directly below where **Babe** stands. Open it with the **Twister** to get 2 Refined Metal.

### 3. Red panel on the giant wreck near the double yellow pipes

Before entering the east-side pipe, look **northeast** — the giant wreck has a **red panel** in its wall holding 1 Refined Metal.

### Also reported

The wreckage inside the **giant yellow tunnel** and the stretch after it, before the spooky station, is reported to hold some — sweep it while passing.

## What refined metal is used for

| Use | Refined Metal needed |
| --- | --- |
| **Gas Tank** (Wires x4 + Refined Metal x2 + Rubber x2 + Aluminum x4) | x2 |
| **Oxygen Candle** (Oxyworm x1 + Battery x1 + Refined Metal x1) | x1 |
| **Spraytron** (Titanium x4 + Refined Metal x4 + Battery x2 + Glass x2) | x4 |
| **Oxygen Cylinder** (Bellyplastic x2 + Titanium x4 + Refined Metal x4 + Aluminum x4) | x4 |

**First pickup unlocks the Gas Tank blueprint.**

## Respawn

Fixed pickups — no refresh documented for the current build. Early Access supplies are extremely scarce: save them and double-check the recipe names in the crafting panel before spending.

> ⚠️ Early Access values may change with updates.

## FAQ

### 1. Can you smelt Metal into Refined Metal?
No. They are separate resources — the "4 Metal → 1 Refined Metal" recipe is a misreading of the Spraytron recipe.

### 2. Where is Refined Metal in Breathedge 2?
Below the antenna platform (1), in the secret stash under the train station's west side (2, Twister), and in the red panel on the giant wreck near the double yellow pipes (1).

### 3. What unlocks when you first find Refined Metal?
The Gas Tank blueprint.

### 4. Does Refined Metal respawn?
Not documented — treat every pickup as one-time.
