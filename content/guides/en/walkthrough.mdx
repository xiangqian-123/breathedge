## Quick answer

**Breathedge 2's Early Access story is Chapter 1 — 13 main quests, and this is the order they unlock.** After the opening (eat, refill oxygen), craft the Crowbar (Metal x2), then the Thumper and the Cutter, and finish by crafting the Backpack (Aluminum x4, Plastic x2, Titanium x4, Electrical Tape x4). Every step below links to a full guide for that objective.

Chapter 1 also has **16 side quests**, most of which pay out an achievement and a permanent stat bonus.

## What to do next

After you craft the **Backpack** (step 13), the Chapter 1 main story is complete. Here's what's left to do:

| After the main story | What it gets you |
|---|---|
| Complete the **16 side quests** | Achievements + stat bonuses |
| Craft the remaining tools (**Spraytron**, **Wrench**) | Full tool kit |
| Collect all materials & unlock every recipe | Full crafting blueprint set |
| Wait for **Chapter 2** | More story (Early Access) |

## Tips

- Hunger drains faster in cold areas — stock up on food or raise the temperature first.
- The turret works like a traffic light: stand still when it looks at you (crouch), move when it turns away.

> ⚠️ Early Access only has Chapter 1 so far; more chapters come with updates.
