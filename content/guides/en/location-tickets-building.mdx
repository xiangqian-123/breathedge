## Quick answer

The ticket building near the station — home of the Ticket Booth quest, patrolled by a turret.

## Quests here

- [Ticket Booth](/en/guide/ticket-booth) — the repair quest with a [three-knob puzzle](/en/guide/problem-ticket-booth-puzzle)

## Materials here

- [Metal](/en/guide/material-metal), [Electrical Tape](/en/guide/material-electrical-tape), [Electronics](/en/guide/material-electronics) — used to repair the booth

## Achievements here

- [Public enemy no. 1](/en/guide/achievement-public-enemy-no-1) — the donut sign

## Danger

Turret (traffic-light mechanic — stand still when it turns red)
