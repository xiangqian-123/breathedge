## Quick answer

The attendant platform near the station, to the left (may be frozen before power is restored).

## Location

The attendant platform near the station, to the left (may be frozen before power is restored).

## Materials needed

None (find the body parts).

## Steps

1. Find the attendant platform — the robot is missing head, torso and arm
2. Head: floating near the oxygen station to the left
3. Torso: underneath the rocks below the platform
4. Arm: floating above the yellow/white bridge structures to the right
5. Place the pieces on the base to reassemble

## Reward

Attendant achievement + Physical Protection +2.
