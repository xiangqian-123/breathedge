## Quick answer

Fix the **Service Warmers** inside the **Depot** to raise the area's **Ambient Temperature to 100%**. Ambient Temperature is the *area's* heat — it is **not** the same as your suit's **Cold Protection**, which is a separate stat.

## Cold Protection vs Ambient Temperature

These are two different systems, and mixing them up is the most common confusion:

- **Ambient Temperature** — the heat of the area you're standing in. Raised by repairing Service Warmers.
- **Cold Protection** — your suit's resistance to cold. A separate stat, raised through equipment.

**Reaching 100% on one does not fill the other.**

## Depot location & route

The Depot is reached after you unlock the **Twister** (from the [Three Antennas quest](/en/guide/quest-three-antennas)). Enter the Depot and work through it, repairing each accessible warmer as you go — every repair pushes the Ambient Temperature higher.

## Ambient Temperature progression

There's no single "fix one thing" step — repair **each accessible Service Warmer** in the Depot and the Ambient Temperature climbs until the journal confirms the objective is complete.

## Cold Protection 100

If your journal shows a **Cold Protection** goal, that's your suit stat, not the Depot temperature. Raise it through equipment — don't confuse it with the warmer repairs.

## 0.8.11 Easy-difficulty fix

On **Easy** difficulty, the cold-related task used to wrongly require **130** — patch 0.8.11 fixed it so **100** completes the objective. If you're on Easy and it feels stuck, update the game.

## Depot leaking / still cold (FAQ)

### Temperature won't reach 100
You missed a warmer — sweep the Depot for every accessible warmer, not just the ones on the main path.

### I have the materials but can't repair
You may be missing the **Twister** (from Three Antennas), or you haven't advanced the story far enough.

### "Depot leaking" or other oddities
Early-access quirks — most are fixed by updating to the latest patch.

## Next: 10G Antenna
