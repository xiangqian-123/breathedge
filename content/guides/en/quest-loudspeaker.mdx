## Quick answer

The disconnected loudspeaker **floats in space behind/over the train, near the mole-burrow asteroid cluster**. Carry it back to the train, mount it, repair it with **Electrical Tape x3 + Metal x2**, then switch it on.

## Location

Fly **over the train** and search the asteroid cluster near the **mole burrows** — the speaker is floating in space there, not attached to anything. Some guides describe the same spot as "turn around and look behind the asteroid marked with a red circle".

If you can't spot it right away, sweep the asteroids just behind the train — the speaker is a small floating object, easy to pass by.

> Patch note: Hotfix 0.8.5 fixed this quest's objective marker — the marker now points to the **oxygen terminal** during the repair step. If your HUD shows a confusing marker, that's why.

## Materials needed

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Electrical Tape | x3 |
| Metal | x2 |

## Steps

1. Fly over the train and pick up the floating loudspeaker near the mole-burrow asteroids
2. Carry it back to the train and attach it to its **mounting point**
3. Repair it with Electrical Tape x3 + Metal x2
4. Interact with it **once more to switch it on** — the quest completes at this step

## Reward

No stat or achievement reward. The real payoff: the repaired speaker **increases Oxyworm spawns in the area**, which feeds the [Oxyworm](/en/guide/material-oxyworm) collection achievement (300 Oxyworms).

## FAQ

### 1. Where is the Loudspeaker in Breathedge 2?
Floating in space behind the train, near the mole-burrow asteroid cluster. Look for a small floating object, not something attached to the train.

### 2. What materials does the Loudspeaker need?
Electrical Tape x3 and Metal x2.

### 3. I repaired the speaker but the quest didn't complete.
Interact with it one more time to switch it on. Repairing is not the last step.

### 4. Why does the objective marker look wrong?
Hotfix 0.8.5 changed the marker to the oxygen terminal during the repair step — the speaker itself is still out by the asteroids.
