## Quick answer

**4 Metal smelts into 1 Refined Metal** (multi-source confirmed). It can also be looted from deep industrial equipment.

## Steps

1. Stockpile 4 Metal
2. Smelt 4 Metal into 1 Refined Metal
3. Or dismantle deep industrial equipment to loot it directly
