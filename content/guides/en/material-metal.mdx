## Quick answer

Orange metal wreckage floating in space; also drops from breaking barrels and metal containers.

## How to get it

Break down wreckage and containers with the crowbar.

## What it's used for

Scrap (2), Cutter (4), Thumper (4), Purified Oxygen (1), Oxygen Station repair (3); 4 Metal smelts into 1 Refined Metal.
