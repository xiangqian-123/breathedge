Breathedge 2 entered **Early Access on August 31, 2026** on Steam and the Epic Games Store.

## Key timeline

- **2025**: the demo launched at Steam Next Fest to positive reception, but the developers later **rebuilt it from scratch**.
- **Aug 31, 2026**: Early Access launch, with only Chapter 1 plus one large explorable area.
- **1.0 full release**: date unannounced. The developers expect Early Access to last roughly **16-20 months**, but say that window may move.

## What Early Access includes

The launch build includes: Chapter 1 of the main story, a large explorable area, survival and open-space exploration, resource gathering, crafting, upgrades, repairs, combat, main and side quests, NPCs, and plenty of optional locations and events.

## Planned updates

During Early Access the developers plan to add through major updates:

- The main story continuing
- New areas, including **planetary surface** gameplay
- More characters and quests
- Vehicle systems (small flying/ground vehicles, a personal spacecraft, a warp-jump system)
- Building

> ⚠️ The launch is Early Access, not the final game — saves, balance, and UI may change with updates.
