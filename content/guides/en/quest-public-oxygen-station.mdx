## Quick answer

Across from the attendant platform, left of the train platform.

## Location

Across from the attendant platform, left of the train platform.

## Materials needed

Metal x3, Electronics x2, Wires x1, Electrical Tape x3.

## Steps

1. Locate the public oxygen station
2. Repair it with the materials above
3. Use it up close afterward; fill with ice to keep it supplied

## Reward

A permanent oxygen refill point.
