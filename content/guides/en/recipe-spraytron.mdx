## Quick answer

The **Spraytron** is the watergun-style multi-tool. Recipe: **Titanium x4, Refined Metal x4, Battery Cell x2, Glass x2**. The recipe unlocks the moment you **pick up a Spraytron** for the first time.

## Recipe

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Titanium | x4 |
| Refined Metal | x4 |
| Battery Cell | x2 |
| Glass | x2 |

> Early Access — confirm the exact amounts on your own crafting screen; the in-game panel is final.

## How to unlock

Pick up a **Spraytron** for the first time — the recipe unlocks on pickup (not through a quest or resource collection).

## What the Spraytron does

| Use | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Clean the shuttle** | Signal Jammer main quest — step in the shuttle sequence |
| **Extract Alkali** | Alkaline Mole burrows (yield 3-5 per deposit) |
| **Spray Alkaline Solution** | uses Alkaline Solution as a liquid charge |

## Repair

Community reports it's repaired with **Aluminum x1, Wires x1, Plastic x1** — confirm on the in-game repair panel.

## FAQ

### Where do I get a Spraytron?
It's tied to the Signal Jammer main quest. The recipe unlocks when you first pick one up — craft one at the crafting menu from then on.

### What is the Spraytron for?
Two jobs: **cleaning the shuttle** (Jammer quest) and **extracting Alkali** from Mole burrows.
