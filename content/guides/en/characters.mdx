Breathedge 2 continues the first game's universe — the protagonist and a cast of absurd companions fight the Breathedge Corporation. Here are the confirmed characters.

## Protagonist & companions

| Character | Notes |
|------|------|
| **The Man** | The protagonist, an ordinary survivor who solves problems with scrap, duct tape, and questionable machines |
| **Suit** | Your companion AI, always wisecracking, returns with an upgradable mechanical grabbing arm |
| **Chicken** | The immortal Chicken, series mascot, always along for the ride |

## NPCs & robots

| Character | Notes |
|------|------|
| **Babe** | Key main-quest NPC who needs you to fix the window and find tape |
| **Attendant** | A damaged robot near the train station; repairing her grants a Physical Protection bonus |
| **Coffinbot** | Carried over from the first game |
| **Bum** | Tied to the "Battle for the Mattress" encounter; defeat him for a Max Health bonus |

## Unconfirmed (Early Access details not fully verified)

| Character | Notes |
|------|------|
| **Foreman** | Character name seen in playthroughs, tied to equipment calibration; EA details TBC |
| **Chuck** | The taxi driver robot from the original demo's taxi quest; whether he returns in EA is TBC |

## Enemies & creatures

In Chapter 1 you'll meet flying worms, spitting moles, screaming larvae, and alkaline moles, plus repair bots, police bots, and scavenger robots. Robots patrol on their own routes, chase or flee, and are a solid (but risky) source of resources.

> ⚠️ Early Access characters and quest lines may expand with updates; TBC entries are as per the current build.
