## Quick answer

Electronics come from **red electrical panels around the station** (many marked with a yellow warning triangle) and from **dismantling appliances** — the washing machine near the starting structure is the classic first pick. Some panels open directly; some need a **Scrap** hit.

## Where to find electronics

- **Red electrical panels around the train station** — the most reliable source
- **Appliance teardown**: TVs, washing machines, broken machinery — inside the station and wrecks rather than open space
- Some crates and containers hold a few

## How to collect

Some panels open directly; others need the **Scrap** to break open. Dismantling appliances follows the same logic — interact, and hit it with Scrap when the prompt says so.

> One guide mentions a "Sui Hand" tool for pulling parts — likely a translation artifact, treat as unverified.

## What electronics are used for

- **Service Warmer repairs** (Electronics x2)
- **Various machine repairs**: antenna, generator, terminals

> Note: one full recipe table lists no Electronics ingredients at all — likely an incomplete source. The Service Warmer x2 figure comes from a single guide; both are reflected here for transparency.

## Player advice

Early repairs consume Electronics fast. **Don't spend them on anything optional — pick up every panel you see.** Think of Electronics like Wires: always scarce, always needed.

## Respawn

Not confirmed for the current build. Sweep every panel and appliance you pass.
