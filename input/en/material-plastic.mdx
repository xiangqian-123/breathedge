## Quick answer

Plastic comes from **gray spheres on the spiked poles at platform edges**. The first one is **right above your own train** — climb onto the roof and look toward the front. Break them with the **Scrap** (`Break` prompt, a few hits each).

## Where to find plastic

- **Spiked poles at platform edges** — gray spheres at the tips; the most reliable source. The first sits directly above your train car.
- **Glass corridor** north of the station (the hallway with windows everywhere) — intact plastic spheres, rarer
- **Near the giant yellow pipes**, beside the train car cut in half (northwest of the station, next to a floating astronaut corpse)
- Hardened gray plastic lumps on wreck ships
- Chinese guides: plastic is everywhere, but **densest around the starting station** and in the stretch after the yellow pipe corridor, before the spooky station

One guide maps up to 5 plastic sources totaling about 10 units — enough for early crafting and repairs.

## What plastic looks like

Two descriptions coexist — likely two spawn types:

- **Gray sphere** at the tip of a spiked pole (GameRant)
- **Dark lumpy growth** attached to pipes, broken parts, hull plates and platform sides (AllThings)

Identify by shape, not color, and grab both.

## How to collect

**Scrap** (the Crowbar) with the `Break` prompt — takes a few hits to shatter. (One guide says some sources respond to the Cutter — if Break does nothing, try Cut.)

## What plastic is used for

| Use | Plastic needed |
| --- | --- |
| **Thumper** (Metal x4 + Plastic x2) | x2 |
| **Cutter** (Aluminum x4 + Plastic x2 + Metal x4) | x2 |
| **Twister** (Aluminum x4 + Wires x2 + Electric Tape x3 + Plastic x2) | x2 |
| **Flashlight** (Plastic x2 + Wires x2 + Glass x2 + Light Bulb x1) | x2 |
| **Backpack** (Aluminum x4 + Plastic x2 + Titanium x4 + Electric Tape x4) | x2 |
| Nutri-Pack-O-Mat repair | x1 |

**Break plastic on sight** — it's essential for nearly every early tool and the Backpack.

> Bellyplastic is a different item (dropped by the Oxyworm larvae on the CZNX boss at the Old Station, main story).

## Respawn

No respawn mechanic documented. Emptied nodes stay emptied — move to the next pole, come back later if you want the same spots (they won't run away, but they won't regrow either).

## FAQ

Gray spheres on the spiked poles at platform edges — the first is right above your train.

The Scrap (Crowbar) with the Break prompt. A few hits per sphere.
