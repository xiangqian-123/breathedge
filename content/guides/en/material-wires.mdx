## Quick answer

Wires come from **junction boxes and red-white electrical panels** — the boxes with multicolor cables sticking out. Use the **Cutter** (`Cut` prompt). Only targets that show an interaction prompt actually give wires — visible cables without a prompt are scenery.

## Where to find wires

- **Junction boxes** with multicolored cables sticking out
- **Red-white electrical panels** on platforms, wreckage, ships and stations — check maintenance areas
- Near the train is your first encounter; later, exposed cables and interactable machinery
- Rarely in crates or floating in open space

## How to collect

Equip the **Cutter** and use **Cut** (not Break, not Drill) on the box or panel. Any Cutter type works, but you must craft one first.

## What wires are used for

| Use | Wires needed |
| --- | --- |
| **Twister** (Aluminum x4 + Wires x2 + Electric Tape x3 + Plastic x2) | x2 |
| **Flashlight** (Plastic x2 + Wires x2 + Glass x2 + Light Bulb x1) | x2 |
| **Gas Tank** (Wires x4 + Refined Metal x2 + Rubber x2 + Aluminum x4) | x4 |
| **Service Warmer repair** (Metal x4 + Light Bulb x1 + Wires x4 + Electronics x2) | x4 |
| Nutri-Pack-O-Mat repair | x2 |

Plenty of other recipes and repairs use them — treat wires as a permanent shopping-list item.

## Respawn

Not confirmed. Early Access spawn behavior is incompletely documented — don't assume dismantled objects come back.

## Player advice

Supplies look fine at first and vanish fast. **Cut panels along the way and hoard wires** — backtracking for them wastes oxygen.

## FAQ

### 1. Where is Wires in Breathedge 2?
Junction boxes and red-white electrical panels with multicolor cables — platforms, wrecks, ships, stations.

### 2. Why can't I get wires from the cables I see?
Visible cables aren't targets. Only boxes/panels that show an interaction prompt give wires.

### 3. What tool do I need for Wires?
The Cutter — the prompt reads Cut.

### 4. Do Wires respawn?
Not confirmed — cut panels as you pass, don't plan on refills.
