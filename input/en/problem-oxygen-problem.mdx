## Quick answer

**Three ways to refill: repair the Public Oxygen Station (field refill point), craft Purified Oxygen (Oxyworm x2 + Metal x1 + Ice x1), or suck Oxyworms directly.** Repair the station before long trips.

## Steps

1. Repair the Public Oxygen Station first (Metal x3 + Electronics x2 + Wires x1 + Electrical Tape x3)
2. Carry some Purified Oxygen
3. Suck Oxyworms when you see them in the field
