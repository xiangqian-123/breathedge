## Quick answer

Discovered while repairing the service warmers or heading to the 10G antenna.

## Location

Discovered while repairing the service warmers or heading to the 10G antenna.

## Materials needed

None.

## Steps

1. Approach a nest and suck oxygen out of it
2. Find 5 different nests to complete the task

## Reward

Unlocks new crafting recipes (including portable oxygen).
