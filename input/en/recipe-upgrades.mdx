## Quick answer

Tools are upgraded with modules at the **Workbench**; the spacesuit is modified at the **Wardrobe**. Modules are removable and recyclable, and disappear when broken.

## Tool upgrades

Only Scrap, Cutter and Thumper have documented upgrade slots among the early Chapter 1 tools.

| Tool | Module | Slot | Stat bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scrap | Gear | Heft | Damage +10 |
| Scrap | Barbell Plate | Heft | Damage +20 |
| Cutter | Scissors | Edge | Damage +5 |
| Cutter | Jaws | Edge | Damage +10 |
| Thumper | Scissors | Edge | Damage +12 |
| Thumper | Jaws | Edge | Damage +15 |

Scrap can also accept Handle-type modules.

## Station upgrades

Both Kettle Bobby and the Nutri-Pack-O-Mat can be improved using scavenged parts.

| Station | Module | Slot | Stat bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kettle Bobby | Foam | Filter | Processing +0.5 |
| Kettle Bobby | Cardboard | Filter | Processing +0.3 |
| Kettle Bobby | Knitted Teddy Bear | Filter | Processing +0.6 |
| Kettle Bobby | Backpack | Container | Capacity +30 |
| Nutri-Pack-O-Mat | Plastic Barrel | Container | Capacity +50 |

## Tip

- Modules are removable parts, not permanent upgrades: check before discarding unusual junk — it might be a module.
- For raw damage, the Barbell Plate (+20) is the strongest; Jaws is the best Edge module for Cutter and Thumper.

> ⚠️ Data from the games.gg human-authored guide; Early Access values may change with updates.
