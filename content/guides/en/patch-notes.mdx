## Quick answer

The current version is **0.8.10** (Sep 23, 2026). Since the Aug 31 Early Access launch the developers have shipped **six patches** (0.8.5 → 0.8.10), mostly fixing quest softlocks, corrupted saves, and survival-stat pacing.

## Full changelog

| Version | Date | What changed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.8.10** | Sep 23, 2026 | Fixed quests (mud statue marker/trajectory, snowman nose count, Spanish taxi letter); fixed multiple stuck points (station admin, antenna parts, Chuck's taxi, toilet door); training dummy collision; Steam Deck UI buttons; encyclopedia/log/character window; Esc sleep interrupt; **reduced the movement-speed penalty from fatigue/overeating/overload debuffs**; fixed mole respawn | [steamdb](https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/25433330/) |
| **0.8.9** | Sep 13, 2026 | Replaced the Suit's English voice (back to the original voice actor); fixed encyclopedia text/links; Steam Deck optimization; fixed 2 more quest states | [bestof.games](https://bestof.games/en/articles/breathedge-2-early-access) |
| **0.8.8** | mid-Sep 2026 | Fixed quest softlocks, the intro save lock, and character stuck spots; **fixed a FatalError caused by the old Intel AI Boost driver on Intel Core Ultra** | [bestof.games](https://bestof.games/en/articles/breathedge-2-early-access) |
| **0.8.7** | Sep 4, 2026 | Reworked door open/close logic to prevent getting stuck; limited saving before the shuttle scene finished; fixed the removable joystick corrupting saves; crowbar can no longer break crates/lampshades/robots/doors (needs the Twister); fixed the "Debug Pornhubble" quest marker; taxi letters now scattered within a 20 m radius | [vgspoilers](https://www.vgspoilers.com/game/breathedge-2/patches/0.8.7) |
| **0.8.6** | early Sep 2026 | Standard-difficulty food/water/fatigue drain **-15%** (responding to "hunger/thirst interrupts exploration" complaints) | [bestof.games](https://bestof.games/en/articles/breathedge-2-early-access) |
| **0.8.5** | Sep 1, 2026 | Fixed quest/control issues (the "Repair the Speaker" marker was moved to the oxygen terminal, etc.) | [vgover](https://www.vgover.com/news/233231) |

## Upcoming content

The patch cadence is fast because the game is Early Access. Planned major updates (undated): small vehicles, then base building + planetary landing/travel, then a personal spacecraft and warp-jump system.
