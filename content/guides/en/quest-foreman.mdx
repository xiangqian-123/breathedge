## Quick answer

Repair the **sparking foreman robot** halfway to the third antenna/memorial (beside a service warmer) with Metal x4 + Electronics x3, then answer its 3 test questions — this fixes the nearby service warmer.

## Location

The sparking foreman robot halfway to the third antenna/memorial (beside a service warmer).

## Materials needed

[Metal](/en/guide/material-metal) x4, [Electronics](/en/guide/material-electronics) x3.

## Steps

1. Approach and press M2 to discharge the current
2. Provide the materials to repair
3. Answer the three test questions (fast & cheap / when the inspector shows up / every hour)

## Reward

Repairs the nearby [service warmer](/en/guide/quest-service-warmers).
