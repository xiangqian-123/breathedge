## Quick answer

Craft the **Thumper** (Metal x4 + Plastic x2), leave the train, and mine **orange-silver patches on large rocks** in the Outskirts asteroid belt. **Don't search the small loose scrap around the train** — Aluminum is on big asteroids only.

## Where to find aluminum

**The Outskirts asteroid belt** — straight out from the train/base exit. Focus on **large floating rocks**, not debris or small scrap. Exact coordinates are deliberately not published by guides (and unverified) — sweep the big rocks.

## What aluminum looks like

**Orange-silver patches** blended into the gray asteroid surface — from a distance they read as ordinary rock texture. Fly within a few meters until the **"Aluminum deposits" label + Drill prompt** appears. That prompt is the only reliable way to spot a vein.

## Required tool: Thumper

The **Thumper** (the drill) is mandatory:

- Equip it, approach the deposit, and **hold to mine**
- **Crowbar and Cutter do nothing** to Aluminum deposits — that's the #1 reason players think aluminum is missing

**Getting the Thumper from scratch**: pick up 2 Metal near the train → craft the **Crowbar** (Metal x2) → pry marked containers for Metal and Plastic → craft the **Thumper** (Metal x4 + Plastic x2).

## What aluminum is used for

| Use | Aluminum needed |
| --- | --- |
| **Cutter** (Aluminum x4 + Plastic x2 + Metal x4) | x4 |
| **Twister** (Aluminum x4 + Wires x2 + Electric Tape x3 + Plastic x2) | x4 |
| **Gas Tank** (Wires x4 + Refined Metal x2 + Rubber x2 + Aluminum x4) | x4 |
| **Backpack** (Aluminum x4 + Plastic x2 + Titanium x4 + Electric Tape x4) | x4 |

That's **16 total** for the core set. Priority: **Cutter first** (it gates train repairs), then keep 4 for the Twister once it unlocks.

## Can't mine aluminum?

1. **Thumper not equipped?** Crowbar/Cutter don't work on deposits
2. **Node already depleted?** Fly to the next large rock
3. **Too far away?** The Drill prompt only appears within a few meters
4. **Can't see any veins?** Slow down and orbit the rock — the patches hide in the gray texture
5. **Oxygen?** Bring Ice or Purified Oxygen, don't leave with just enough air to return

> ⚠️ Per-node yield (guides say 2) and respawn timing are unverified for the current build — don't plan around fixed amounts.

## FAQ

### 1. Where is Aluminum in Breathedge 2?
The Outskirts asteroid belt — orange-silver patches on large rocks, not the small scrap near the train.

### 2. Why can't I mine Aluminum?
You need the Thumper. Crowbar and Cutter don't work on aluminum deposits.

### 3. How do I spot an Aluminum vein?
Fly close to large asteroids until the "Aluminum deposits" + Drill prompt appears — the patches look like rock texture from afar.

### 4. How much Aluminum do I need?
16 for the core set: Cutter x4, Twister x4, Gas Tank x4, Backpack x4.

### 5. Does Aluminum respawn?
Not confirmed — mine new rocks instead of waiting.
