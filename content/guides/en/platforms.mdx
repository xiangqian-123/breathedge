| Platform | Status |

| **Epic Games Store (Windows)** | ✅ Live (Early Access) |

## Features

- **Single-player**: strictly solo, no co-op or multiplayer (confirmed by the developers).
- **Controller support**: full Xbox controller support.
- **Family Sharing**: supported on Steam.

## Supported languages

- **Audio**: English, Russian
- **Interface / subtitle text** (13): English, French, German, Spanish (Spain / Latin America), Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, **Simplified Chinese**, Turkish, Ukrainian

12+ (mild swearing, moderate violence, sexual innuendo).
