Breathedge 2 is in Early Access with a **gradual price increase** plan — this is the key to deciding whether to buy now or wait.

## Current price & launch discount

Official US Steam price (as of Sep 1, 2026):

- **Base price**: $19.99
- **Launch discount**: -20%, bringing it to **$15.99**
- **Offer ends**: Sep 14, 2026

> Regional pricing varies (e.g. Epic shows SGD 20.75 / 16.60 in Singapore) — check your local store page.

## The important part: the price will rise

The developers state in the Early Access FAQ:

- Like the first game, **the price will increase at full release**.
- It will **likely rise gradually with major updates**.
- They "don't intend to make Breathedge 2 an expensive game."

**Conclusion**: buy during Early Access (especially the launch discount) if you want the best price — it only gets more expensive later.

## Platform price reference

- **Steam**: roughly the same as Epic, with the same launch discount.
- **Third-party CD key sites**: sometimes cheaper, but watch for channel risk.

## Buying advice

- Want Chapter 1 now and to support development → buy during the launch discount.
- Want the full story and stable balance → wait for 1.0, but expect a higher price.
