## Quick answer

The broken sandstone statue through the yellow tunnels, toward the 10G antenna.

## Location

The broken sandstone statue through the yellow tunnels, toward the 10G antenna.

## Materials needed

None (find the statue pieces).

## Steps

1. Find the sandstone statue and collect 6 pieces to reassemble (ear, eye x2, finger, mouth, nose, scattered around the statue)

## Reward

Leader achievement + Gas Reserve +20.
