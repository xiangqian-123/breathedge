## Quick answer

Locate the damaged Leader statue and return its missing colored floating pieces until the statue has been rebuilt.

## Achievement info

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Leader |
| Global unlock rate | 34.5% |
| Category | Chapter 1 |
| Stat reward | Gas Reserve +20 |

## Official description

> The Leader can do no wrong; it's the underlings who are the problem. Look at them, flying around instead of working.

## Reward

Unlocking it permanently grants **Gas Reserve +20**. Achievement bonuses stack, and their effects can be checked on the in-game Rewards screen.
