## Quick answer

Refined Metal **can't be smelted** — Metal and Refined Metal are separate resources. Grab the fixed spawns instead.

## Steps

1. **Below the antenna platform**: 1 Refined Metal directly underneath it
2. **Secret stash under the train station**: approach from the **west**, handle below Babe's position, open with the **Twister** → 2 Refined Metal
3. **Red panel on the giant wreck** near the double yellow pipes (look northeast before the east-side pipe) → 1 Refined Metal
4. First pickup unlocks the **Gas Tank** blueprint

> If a guide tells you to smelt 4 Metal into 1 Refined Metal, it's wrong — that's a misreading of the Spraytron recipe, which consumes 4 Refined Metal.
