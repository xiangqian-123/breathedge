## Quick answer

Progress until the area's ambient temperature can be raised, then follow the yellow pipes beneath the station. Repair the gas lamp, clear the ice, locate the missing snowman parts, and rebuild it.

## Achievement info

| Field | Value |

| Category | Chapter 1 |
| Stat reward | Cold Protection +5 |

## Official description

> Building one snowman is basically a full gym workout. Then again, so is literally anything—there's not much to do in a gym anyway.

## Reward

Unlocking it permanently grants **Cold Protection +5**. Achievement bonuses stack, and their effects can be checked on the in-game Rewards screen.
