## The DIY upgrade system

The official upgrade core is the **DIY (Do It Yourself)** system:

- Base tools take **seven module categories**: Sharp, Impact, Utility, Protective, Active, Capacity, and Biological.
- Modules have **rarities and qualities**; the same category works across devices and even repairs world objects.
- **Swappable**: remove and swap modules between tools while they're intact, but you **can't duplicate** them.
- **Breakable but recoverable**: a broken DIY module is gone, but you can recover your favorite one with raw resources; the **base tool stays forever**.
- **Protective armor**: Protective modules act as armor, with different stats for cold, combat, or radiation; a full matching set grants extra bonuses. Armor wears out, so refresh your wardrobe.

> The game's survival motto: "It doesn't matter where your hands grow from, as long as they're golden." The DIY system turns resource gathering from "hoard enough" into "solve the problem."

> ⚠️ Early Access recipes may change — check in-game.
