## Quick answer

Physically enter all seven toilets located around the Outskirts. Simply passing by a toilet does not count, so step inside each one before moving on.

## Reward

Unlocking it permanently grants **Fatigue Drain -5%**. Achievement bonuses stack, and their effects can be checked on the in-game Rewards screen.
