## Quick answer

Spiked-ball nodes on drifting debris (break them open); also found inside capsules.

## How to get it

Smash the spiked nodes with the crowbar.

## What it's used for

Backpack (4). Scarce — save 4 for the Backpack instead of spending them.


## Tip

Titanium is the Backpack's key material and spawns farther out — plan ahead.
