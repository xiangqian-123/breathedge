## Quick answer

Repair the damaged Attendant near the train station. Search the nearby area for her missing body parts and return each piece to the robot's base until she is fully assembled.

Unlocking it permanently grants **Physical Protection +2**. Achievement bonuses stack, and their effects can be checked on the in-game Rewards screen.
