## Quick answer

Your first Battery is **frozen on the station roof** — visible as soon as you turn around. More are in the **ship debris east of the station** and in the **broken luggage compartment near your starting car**.

Don't confuse **Battery** (a large installable unit) with **Battery Cells** (small crafting materials that drop from smashed radios).

## Battery locations

| Location | How to get it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Station roof** | Break the ice first, then pick it up | The one you see almost immediately |
| **Ship debris east of the station** | Fly inside the wreck, grab the floating battery | Slightly below station level; this one is used for the antenna repair |
| **Broken luggage compartment near your starting car** | Fly right, turn right, look down; the battery floats inside | Also holds a collectible |
| **Near the Chuck's Taxi sign** | Floating in space | The most convenient one for the Weird Toilet side quest |
| **Below the repaired sign** | Floating nearby | Used in the Chuck's Taxi "start the drill" step |

> ⚠️ Single-source spots (billboard behind the station, turret battery) are left out — check the areas above first, they're the confirmed ones.

## What a battery looks like

A large blocky unit, usually **encased in ice** on the station roof or **floating** inside wreckage. When you see an **orange-cabled external module**, that's the machine a Battery plugs into.

## Battery cells (small crafting material)

Battery Cells are a different item. Smash **floating radios and speakers** (e.g. near the 10G antenna, Chuck's Taxi or the old station) with the Scrap tool to get them, or find them as random loot.

## What batteries are used for

- **Train power**: plug into the empty slot of the train's **External Module**, then raise the pantograph — this thaws the ice around the station and reactivates dead equipment
- **Antenna repair**: one battery at the antenna base/transformer
- **Weird Toilet side quest**: one battery in the front slot, then break the seal
- **Chuck's Taxi**: the "start the drill" step needs a battery, plus Aluminum x2 + Wire x1 + Electronics x4 + Battery Cells x2; the escape step uses the Twister to unscrew a battery from below
- **Drill blueprint**: 10 Refined Metal + 1 Battery (single source — written "purified metal", which doesn't exist as an item; treat as Refined Metal)

> The Ticket Booth repair does **not** use batteries (Metal x3 + Electrical Tape x2 + Electronics x1).

## Does the battery respawn?

Not confirmed for the current build. The stations' snack vending machines do restock over time, but that's food, not batteries. When you need more, use the other confirmed spots above.

## FAQ

### 1. Where is the first Battery in Breathedge 2?
Frozen on the station roof — break the ice and pick it up.

### 2. Is Battery the same as Battery Cell?
No. Battery is a large installable unit; Battery Cells are small materials from smashed radios and speakers.

### 3. Does the Ticket Booth need a Battery?
No — the booth repair uses Metal, Electrical Tape and Electronics.

### 4. Do batteries respawn?
Not confirmed. Treat each spot as one-time and collect from all the known locations.
